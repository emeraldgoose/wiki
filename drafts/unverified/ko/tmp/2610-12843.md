## Overview

Fewer latent tokens promise cheaper diffusion. Yet aggressive spatial compression creates a reconstruction-generation dilemma: adding channels improves reconstruction but makes generation harder. Our goal is to make extreme spatial compression competitive by shifting this frontier. We introduce APEX, short for Asynchronous Prefix denoising for EXtreme compression, which pairs a hierarchical latent space with asynchronous denoising over channel groups.
