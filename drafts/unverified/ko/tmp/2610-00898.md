## Overview

Biological reasoning models use post-training to connect LLMs to biological inputs, typically representations from a biological foundation model and biological text such as gene and pathway descriptions, functional annotations, and gene lists. Their benchmark accuracy is taken as evidence that the LLM reasons over their biological inputs. We test this assumption in six biological reasoning models across DNA, protein, and single cell tasks.
