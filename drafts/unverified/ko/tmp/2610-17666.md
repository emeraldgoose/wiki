## Overview

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) for multi-turn agents relies on sparse terminal rewards that say little about individual steps. Within-group step comparisons depend on reliable state matching and sufficiently diverse sampled outcomes, while direct step scoring may require costly external evaluators, human annotations, or task-specific heuristics. We introduce DeltaTTG to learn a reusable progress signal through a shared progress meter, using low-cost supervision from diverse policies’ recorded trajectories without task-specific progress rules.
