# Introducing time series collections for the next generation of Amazon OpenSearch Serverless

**Source**: [AWS Big Data Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/big-data/introducing-the-next-generation-of-serverless-time-series-for-amazon-opensearch-service) · **Published**: 2026-10-27

## Overview

Amazon OpenSearch Serverless adds support for time series collections on its next generation, aimed at efficient storage and querying of time-series data without managing infrastructure.

## Key points

- Time series collections are introduced for the next generation of OpenSearch Serverless.
- Supports time-oriented data workloads with serverless operational simplicity.
- Targets cost-efficient storage and querying for time-series use cases.
- Reduces operational overhead compared with self-managed clusters.

## Details

The release extends serverless OpenSearch to time series workloads, balancing performance and management overhead.
