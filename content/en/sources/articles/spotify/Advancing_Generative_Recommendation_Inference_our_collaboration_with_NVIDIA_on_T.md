## Overview

At Spotify, every surface and interaction is personalized to you as a user: the content on the Home page, the next song that plays, and more recently the ability to talk to Spotify. Traditionally, this content is provided by recommendation systems that require multiple steps for candidate retrieval and ranking, and perhaps individual systems per surface. Generative AI is fundamentally changing this. We have been exploring how to provide recommendations via a Large Personalized Language Model in real time, across multiple surfaces.
