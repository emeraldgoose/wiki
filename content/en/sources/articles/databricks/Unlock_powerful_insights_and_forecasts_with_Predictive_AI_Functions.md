# Unlock powerful insights and forecasts with Predictive AI Functions

**Source**: [databricks](https://www.databricks.com/blog/unlock-powerful-insights-and-forecasts-predictive-ai-functions) · **Published**: 2026-10-14

## Overview

Data teams all get asked the same questions: "what will sales be next quarter?," "why did this metric move?," and "is this recent data outside the norm of what we typically see?" Answering these advanced analytics questions usually means handing the work to a data science team to build models and manage infrastructure. This creates a bottleneck where business users wait in line and many questions never even get asked. Predictive AI Functions, now in Beta, remove that bottleneck by letting analysts and business users run advanced analytics like forecasting, driver analysis, and anomaly detection, all through built-in functions backed by state-of-the-art ML models. These SQL-native functions can be called directly in SQL, invoked in Genie with plain-language questions, or embedded in dashboa

## Key points

- Predictive AI Functions let business users and SQL analysts create forecasts, detect anomalies, analyze what's driving a metric, and make predictions — all without advanced ML expertise.
- AI Functions are easy to use in Genie and embed in existing workflows without requiring users to build and maintain models
- The launch includes five functions: an upgraded ai_forecast, plus the new ai_top_drivers, ai_detect_anomalies, ai_predict_value, and ai_predict_class.
- ai_forecast: project a time series into the future
- ai_top_drivers: find which segments most drove a change in a metric
- ai_detect_anomalies: flag outlier rows in temporal data
