## Overview

Today, we’re excited to share a major CustomerLake milestone: Campaign Agents is advancing to Beta and Profile Agents is entering Private Preview. This marks the next stage of product maturity for the Agentic CDP embedded in Databricks, and expands participation to more customers. When we introduced CustomerLake at Data + AI Summit, we outlined a new operating model for marketing: embed your CDP into the governed data and AI platform where your most complete customer context already lives.
