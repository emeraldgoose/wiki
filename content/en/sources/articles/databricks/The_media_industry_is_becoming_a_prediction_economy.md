# The media industry is becoming a prediction economy

**Source**: [databricks](https://www.databricks.com/blog/media-industry-becoming-prediction-economy) · **Published**: 2026-10-26

## Overview

For two decades, media and advertising ran on attention. Impressions, reach and clicks were the currency because they were measurable, but across the industry that currency is losing value. At events such as Advertising Week New York, leaders across media, agencies and adtech/martech are asking not "who saw it?" but "what did it change?"

Behind every answer is the rise of the prediction economy, where advantage comes from knowing what an audience will want before the money is spent, and being able to show what it was worth. Performance shifts from a lagging indicator to a leading one. Agents, measurement, martech/adtech convergence, CTV and retail media all point to the same prerequisite: a unified, governed data and AI foundation.

## Key points

- Measurement has to prove impact. A McKinsey study finds only 3% of CMOs can tie half their marketing to a business outcome, reshaping contracts, adtech products and publisher measurement.
- Martech and adtech are collapsing into one stack: with 90 to 120 tools in a typical enterprise, channel-level reporting no longer suffices.
- CTV and retail media need closed loops for audience intelligence, monetization and cross-platform measurement.
- Context is where prediction starts: complete customer and business context across the data estate, illustrated by PetSmart's 22% lift in salon bookings and 13% lift in auto-ship transactions from activating 75 million loyalty members' data.
- Four Cs decide scale: context (CustomerLake, Genie, Lakebase), control, cost and choice (Unity Gateway), from token maxing to value maxing.
- Leaders should treat governance, cost visibility and portability as design requirements from day one.

## Details

The prediction economy replaces attention with outcomes. Agents move from answering to acting and need trusted, governed data underneath them. Effective measurement connects partner data so the loop closes between marketing and business outcomes.

On the customer side, marketers increasingly want to run marketing where they run the business rather than copying golden profiles into a separate CDP. CustomerLake, an agentic CDP, builds trusted Customer 360 profiles on governed data. Genie lets non-technical users ask questions in plain language over their own metrics. Lakebase keeps live operational state next to analytical data so agent memory and decision history are available at production speed.

Control, cost and choice are handled through Unity Gateway: authentication, auditing and PII guardrails; spend attribution by team, model and application with budgets, rate limits and cheaper model routing; and model choice across providers through one API. The foundation stays portable through open formats and Clean Rooms collaboration without copying data.

Looking ahead, advertising must become an integrated, intent-driven 1:1 experience. Leaders should think big about AI as a growth lever, then aim small on first use cases such as audience planning, activation or measurement orchestration, assembling context, guardrails and agreed impact measurement before expanding.
