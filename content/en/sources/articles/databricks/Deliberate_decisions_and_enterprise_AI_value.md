# Deliberate decisions and enterprise AI value

**Source**: [databricks](https://www.databricks.com/blog/deliberate-decisions-and-enterprise-ai-value)
**Date**: 2026-10-30


## Overview

AI progress is moving quickly. Expectations are rising, experimentation is spreading and organizations are under pressure to show meaningful returns. But the decisions that matter most are rarely about whether AI is possible. They are about where to commit, what to prioritize and how to move forward when the evidence is still incomplete. That is the focus of Deliberate Decisions: How Grounded Leadership Is Advancing the Path to Enterprise AI Value, a report produced by DataIQ in partnership with Databricks. The report brings together perspectives from senior data and AI leaders who are making these choices in real organizations.

## Key points

- Why AI progress needs grounded decisions when readiness, evidence and expectations are uneven.
- What senior leaders are learning about investment, ownership, human judgment and strategic flexibility.
- How 5 leadership disciplines help turn AI ambition into sustained enterprise value.

- Five leadership disciplines emerged to help leaders respond deliberately.
