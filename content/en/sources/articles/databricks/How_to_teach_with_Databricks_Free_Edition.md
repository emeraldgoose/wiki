# How to teach with Databricks Free Edition

**Source**: [Databricks Blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/how-teach-databricks-free-edition) · **Published**: 2026-10-26

## Overview

Databricks Free Edition lets instructors run a full data or AI course on the same platform enterprises use in production, with no cloud contract or license required. It lowers barriers for teaching and learning modern data engineering and AI.

## Key points

- Free Edition provides hands-on access to the Databricks platform for classrooms.
- No cloud contract or license is required to get started.
- Supports teaching data and AI courses consistently with industry practice.
- Helps students learn on production-grade tools.
- Improves accessibility of data/AI education.

## Details

Using Free Edition in teaching aligns coursework with real-world Databricks workflows while keeping cost and setup low.
