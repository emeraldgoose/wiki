# How public sector organizations can govern AI at scale

**Source**: [Databricks Blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/how-public-sector-organizations-can-govern-ai-scale) · **Published**: 2026-11-06

## Overview

Public sector AI projects often stall because governance requirements around citizen data, defense information, budgets, and compliance are not addressed by typical AI tooling. The organizations that progress solve governance centrally before scaling adoption.

This article presents Unity Gateway within Databricks as that central control plane. It supports model choice for different missions, access control by user and team, spending limits and cost attribution, content guardrails, and data residency requirements, while reducing vendor lock-in through multiple providers and external endpoints.

By unifying visibility across models, agents, and tools, governance teams can answer where data goes and what AI costs. Developers retain access to appropriate models, and compliance and budget teams gain the evidence needed to approve broader AI adoption.

## Key points

- Public sector AI adoption is constrained by data sovereignty, security, and budget accountability.
- Governance must answer where data is processed and what AI spend is attributable.
- Centralized governance solves these questions once instead of per team.
- Qualified models can be matched to different missions without one model fitting all use cases.
- Spending limits, hard caps, Smart Routing, and unified traces help control cost and usage.
- Data residency controls can keep requests within a workspace geography.

## Details

The article discusses scenarios such as revenue agencies, health services, benefits agencies, and legacy system modernization. It describes Unity Gateway as built into Databricks, without requiring separate AI gateway infrastructure or multi-year implementation.
