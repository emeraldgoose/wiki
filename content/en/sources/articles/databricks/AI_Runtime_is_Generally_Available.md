## Overview

Today, we’re excited to announce the General Availability of AI Runtime (AIR), bringing fully managed GPU infrastructure directly into Databricks so customers can train their most demanding models where their enterprise data already lives. Too often, AI teams spend more time securing GPU capacity, managing failures, and waiting for experiments to launch than improving their models. Running GPU infrastructure separately from their Lakehouse adds governance gaps and puts sensitive training data at risk. As coding agents drive more training experiments, these bottlenecks and risks grow — making reliable, fast, and governed infrastructure essential.
