# Azure Databricks sessions at Microsoft Ignite 2026

**Source**: [Databricks Blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/azure-databricks-sessions-microsoft-ignite-2026)
**Published**: 2026-11-17

## Overview

Azure Databricks is a Visionary-level sponsor at Microsoft Ignite 2026, November 17–20 at Moscone Center in San Francisco, with booth #5271R in The Hub (Nov 17–19) and executive meetings at The Social @ Canopy Hotel (Nov 17–18, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM PT). The theme: Build, Govern and Run AI that's yours on Azure Databricks — Genie in Microsoft Teams, Excel and Microsoft Copilot Cowork, all governed by Unity Catalog — helping organizations move from AI pilots to production AI.

## Key points

- Featured session: "Build governed AI that understands your business context" — Wednesday, Nov 18, 2:15 PM PT, Robert Saxby (Databricks) and Michel Porter (EY); EY's assurance practice ingests, transforms and governs billions of records for audits, with a live demo of Genie in Teams and Copilot Cowork.
- Azure Databricks for government cloud: modern data and AI for Azure Government (lightning talk, Nov 17, 2:00 PM) — public-sector lakehouse, governance, sovereignty.
- Fine-tune NVIDIA Nemotron models with Azure Databricks AI Runtime (Nov 18, 1:20 PM) — hands-on post-training with NeMo, receipt-extraction multimodal example, serverless GPU, security/governance considerations.
- Scale agentic AI and turn data into autonomous action (Nov 18, 1:45 PM) — Diego Jarne (PwC), Bernhard Pfirrmann (Nokia), Eric Tranby (CareQuest), Corrinne Walkaus (Markel) on Azure OpenAI + Azure Databricks + lakehouse for agentic AI in life sciences, insurance, telecom.
- What's new in Azure Storage (Nov 18, 3:00 PM) — David Meyer (Databricks) with Microsoft on ADLS and OneLake for AI-ready data foundations.
- Manage and secure AI agents at enterprise scale with Agent 365 (Nov 18, 4:15 PM) — control plane for agents: visibility, built-in governance, enterprise-grade security.
- Powering AI with Azure Databricks, Foundry, Fabric and Copilot (Nov 19, 10:15 AM) — David Meyer and Anavi Nahar (Microsoft) on OneLake, Foundry, Copilot Studio, Power BI, Teams, M365 integrations.
- Also: Databricks + AMD + Replit customer reception (Nov 17, 6:00–8:00 PM, Mariposa); Databricks leaders available for private meetings Nov 17–18; no Ignite pass required for the executive meetings.

## Details

- Event logistics: Moscone Center; booth #5271R (The Hub, Nov 17–19); executive meetings and the Canopy Hotel social Nov 17–18; Databricks sessions span business leaders, data professionals, IT leaders and developers.
- The lineup emphasizes governed AI: Unity Catalog governance underneath Genie, Copilot Cowork, and Microsoft 365 experiences, plus government-cloud and FedRAMP-track offerings for regulated workloads.
- Microsoft-led sessions covering adjacent ground (Observability at every layer, Work IQ, Cobalt 200, subsurface data) are also listed in the post for attendees planning their agenda.
