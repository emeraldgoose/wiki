# Brickster Voices: Meet Alasdair Anderson

**Source**: [Databricks Blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/brickster-voices-meet-alasdair-anderson) · **Published**: 2026-11-11

## Overview

This Brickster Voices interview sits down with Alasdair Anderson, a Lead Solutions Architect on the Databricks Field Engineering team, the group that helps customers build and scale AI solutions. His path is unusual for a field engineer: two decades on the customer side of financial services before joining Databricks, a company he has known since its founding — including discussing investments with chairman Ion Stoica back in 2014. What converted him was hands-on use: tasks that took days or weeks on traditional stacks ran in seconds or minutes, and he joined to help solve the world's toughest data problems at scale.

A typical week mixes building and coaching. On any given day he might code an app to solve a customer challenge, scale that application for another account, or partner directly with executive management on a business case — switching fluidly between player and coach. He notes that platforms like Lakebase high-scale OLTP keep expanding the team's addressable scope, but the mission is unchanged: delivering measurable impact to customers as a connected team.

The interview's centerpiece anecdote shows the pace of that work. At a top European asset manager, a boardroom AI discussion started with the question "What is a token?" Within 20 minutes on Databricks, Anderson demonstrated how to analyze private credit risks, set warning indicators, and use Document Intelligence AI to identify hidden contagion risks across debt covenants — completely reshaping the board's perspective on what is possible with real-time risk modeling and portfolio hedging. He credits deep preparation (annual reports, analyst guidance, industry news) for being able to meet customers where they are, in line with the company's "Customer Obsessed" and "Truth-Seeking" principles.

## Key points

- The player-coach rhythm: senior field engineers balance building technology with coaching colleagues, moving between coding apps for customer challenges, scaling them across accounts, and working directly with executive management on business cases.
- Twelve months in, tools like Agent Bricks, Lakebase, and AI Apps transformed his financial-services work from optimizing existing processes to completely redesigning them on Databricks.
- The boardroom demo: from "What is a token?" to private credit risk analysis, warning indicators, and Document Intelligence AI surfacing hidden contagion risks across debt covenants — in 20 minutes, at a top European asset manager.
- Cultural principles in practice: "Customer Obsessed" and "Truth-Seeking" translate into deep preparation — annual reports, analyst guidance, industry news — to surface key risks and opportunities before walking into the room.
- Collaboration model: moving from traditional corporate hierarchy to Databricks' open, transparent culture meant aligning EMEA priorities with the GTM FSI leader in month one and executing by month two; FSI SME groups collaborate instantly across time zones (he watched APAC colleagues present a custom demo to a Tokyo customer in Japanese).
- Why financial services work stands apart: the Apache Spark heritage paired with Unity Catalog and Unity Gateway enables complex AI/ML workloads with full governance and control — critical for risk management across credit, market, underwriting, and compliance.
- Where the industry is moving: banks transition from annual KYC checks to real-time "Perpetual KYC"; insurers deploy agentic AI in claims processing to cut costs while boosting customer NPS; global trading institutions run liquidity calculations every 5 minutes instead of twice daily during peak geopolitical volatility.
- Scale of engagements: Databricks customers process trillions of dollars daily and insure risk equal to national GDPs; executive teams rely on Databricks to power core operations and guide multi-billion-dollar strategic changes.
- Community as infrastructure: FSI Slack channels act as a real-time pulse where global teams solve niche, complex customer challenges daily; openness and radical transparency let anyone reach out and collaborate with confidence.
- Career advice: gain domain expertise by steering major business transformations, keep technical fundamentals sharp, align technical projects to overarching business goals, read widely, and seek to understand human incentives — strategic decisions are always made by people.

## Details

- On the switch from the customer side: he had known Databricks since its founding and discussed investments with chairman Ion Stoica in 2014, but hands-on product use — days-or-weeks tasks finishing in seconds or minutes — is what drove the move.
- On what separates solutions architects who excel: there is no single mold. Some become deep technical specialists in products like Unity Gateway or Genie; others become trusted vertical experts. The common denominator is trust — from customers, partners, and peers.
- The interview closes with a hiring note: the Field Engineering team is expanding, and readers are pointed to open roles.
- The piece is part of the Brickster Voices series, which profiles the Databricks Field Engineering team; the opening framing notes enterprises are replacing slow, legacy data stacks to eliminate data silos and overcome complex risk modeling, governance, and compliance workflows.
