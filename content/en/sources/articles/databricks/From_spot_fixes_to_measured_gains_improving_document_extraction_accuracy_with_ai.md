# From spot fixes to measured gains: improving document extraction accuracy with ai_extract and MLflow Prompt Registry

**Source**: [Databricks Blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/spot-fixes-measured-gains-improving-document-extraction-accuracy-aiextract-and-mlflow-prompt) · **Published**: 2026-10-27

## Overview

Document extraction often starts with a simple loop: spot an incorrect field, adjust the prompt or schema, rerun the document, and check. Over time this spot-fix approach becomes unmeasurable. Databricks combines ai_extract and MLflow Prompt Registry to turn ad hoc fixes into measured gains.

## Key points

- Spot fixes for extracted fields are hard to track and measure.
- ai_extract plus MLflow Prompt Registry enables versioned prompt changes.
- Measured gains replace anecdotal improvements with evaluation metrics.
- Supports iterative extraction quality improvements.
- Better governance and reproducibility for extraction pipelines.

## Details

The approach standardizes extraction improvement by capturing prompts and measuring performance, making gains explicit and reproducible.
