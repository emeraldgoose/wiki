# Unity Catalog Pages: a governed home for your business knowledge in Genie Ontology

## Overview

Agents guess confidently when business meaning is scattered across Slack, Confluence, and tribal knowledge. Genie Ontology already learns context automatically from dashboards, queries, and tables — and Unity Catalog Pages add the human-curated layer: stewards define authoritative meanings for key terms, entities, and acronyms once, and Genie treats those definitions as source of truth.

## How Pages fit Genie Ontology

Each Unity Catalog semantic plays a distinct role: metric views say how to calculate, Pages say what a concept means, domains say where to look, and certification says what to trust. When a question touches a defined concept (e.g., what counts as an "active customer"), Genie retrieves the Page instead of inferring, and cites it as a clickable source with ownership — turning grounding into a verifiable trail users can inspect.

## Anatomy of a Page and bootstrapping at scale

A Page combines structured fields (owner, synonyms, description) with a rich body of links, images, tables, and references to catalog and workspace objects, organized under domains next to the data it describes, with related assets and cited sources keeping definitions evidence-backed. Nobody must hand-write them all: Genie Code authors Pages from attached sources or MCP-connected tools (Confluence, Slack, Google Docs, GitHub), including bulk import that turns weeks of copy-paste into minutes. Pages is currently in Beta.
