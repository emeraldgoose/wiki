# BigQuery to Databricks: A Strategic Framework

**Source**: [Databricks Blog](https://www.databricks.com/blog/bigquery-databricks-strategic-framework) · **Published**: 2026-10-27

## Overview

Many organizations support a range of data and analytics workloads on BigQuery today but explore ways to bring BI, ETL, and analytics together on a unified platform. This article lays out a strategic framework for moving from BigQuery to Databricks, focusing on migration planning and workload consolidation.

## Key points

- Many teams run mixed BI/ETL/analytics workloads on BigQuery.
- A strategic migration requires workload classification and sequencing.
- Databricks offers unified data engineering, analytics, and AI foundations.
- Consolidation reduces silos and improves governance and interoperability.
- Framework supports incremental migration rather than big-bang replacement.

## Details

The framework guides organizations to assess, prioritize, and migrate workloads while preserving operations and aligning long-term data strategy.
