# Databricks Is Doubling Down On New York City As A Premier AI Talent Hub

**Source**: [databricks](https://www.databricks.com/blog/databricks-doubling-down-new-york-city-premier-ai-talent-hub) · **Published**: 2026-10-19

## Overview

When Databricks opened its New York R&D hub at 5 Bryant Park earlier this year, we made a bet: world-class AI would be built right here in Manhattan. Today, that bet is paying off. Our NYC engineering team is already shaping the future of enterprise AI, from foundational research to the products that put AI to work for the world's largest organizations. Our NYC office is one of the largest concentrations of AI research talent in the city.

## Key points

- Databricks is doubling its New York City footprint at 5 Bryant Park to about 120,000 square feet across five floors. The space adds a dedicated Engineering and Research floor and an Executive Briefing Center.
- New York has the largest tech talent pool in the US. It's also close to Fortune 500 enterprises in financial services, media, healthcare and retail, and its time zone overlaps with talent hubs in Europe and India.
- Our NYC teams cover the full range of enterprise AI: AI research (reinforcement learning, information retrieval, agent evaluation), AI infrastructure like Unity Gateway, and agentic applications like CustomerLake.
