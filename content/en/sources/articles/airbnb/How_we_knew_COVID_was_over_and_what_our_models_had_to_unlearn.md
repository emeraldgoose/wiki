# How we knew COVID was over (and what our models had to unlearn)

**Source**: [airbnb blog](https://airbnb.tech/ai-ml/how-we-knew-covid-was-over-and-what-our-models-had-to-unlearn) · **Published**: 2026-08-20

## Overview

Airbnb's Forecasting Data Science team produces many forecasts the rest of the company plans around, including demand, bookings, and occupancy. COVID-19 broke ordinary assumptions, so models trained on pandemic behavior had to unlearn and return to pre-pandemic baselines once recovery signals appeared. The article explains how the team knew COVID was over and what models had to unlearn.

## Key points

- Forecasts for demand, bookings, and occupancy depend on structural assumptions that COVID invalidated.
- Detecting recovery required separating pandemic effects from normal seasonality and trend.
- Models had to abandon temporary behaviors learned during COVID and relearn stable patterns.
- Forecasting infrastructure and monitoring were essential to recognize the transition.
- Post-pandemic unlearning is as important as learning during the shock.

## Details

The practical lesson is that forecasting systems must track macro regime changes and adapt model assumptions; otherwise predictions keep encoding outdated pandemic behavior.
