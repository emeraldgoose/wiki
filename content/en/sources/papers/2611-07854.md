# Towards Foundation Decision Models: Drex

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.07854](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07854) · **Published**: 2026-11-05
**Authors**: Nischay Dhankhar, Alisher Amantay, Zhanibek Datbayev, Darkhan Akhmetov, Yash Shah, Nurmukhamed Ubaidullayev, Raman Saparkhan, Dos Baha, Abulhair Saparov

## Overview

Autoregressive language models with long chain-of-thought reasoning have become default for many AI applications because they reach high accuracy. In production they can give inconsistent answers, require output parsing, and add cost and latency. Decision models instead return a probability distribution over user-supplied options without autoregressive decoding.

Drex is a family of four open decision models with 8B, 9B, 35B with 3B active, and 27B parameters. Each request contains a state, usually a document, record, or agent context possibly with images, and questions that ask for a choice among options, a yes/no judgment, or a score on an ordered scale. Training combines supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning from adjusted feedback plus vision tuning for Max.

Ablations show RLAF improves accuracy and consistency losses improve robustness to option order. On model comparisons, Max sets a new state of the art among compared decision models on Decision Index 0.4.1 and is statistically tied with d3-turbo and GPT-6 Sol on Decision Bench v4, trailing Claude Opus 5.5 by a small margin while being about an order of magnitude faster. All four models are open source.

## Key points

- Introduces foundation decision models without autoregressive decoding.
- Provides four open models from 8B to 27B parameters.
- Handles option choices, yes/no judgments, and ordered scale scores.
- Combines supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning from adjusted feedback.
- Adds robustness to option reordering through consistency losses.
- Reports accuracy competitive with larger models at much lower latency.

The 51-page paper contains 13 figures and 25 tables. It discusses decision models for document, record, and agent contexts, optionally including images.
