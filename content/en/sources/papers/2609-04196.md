# Puffin-World: Scaling a Unified Multimodal Model with Native 3D World States

Source: Kang Liao and 9 other authors, arXiv:2609.04196, Sep 3, 2026. Project page at kangliao929.github.io/projects/puffin-world/.

**Lead**: Building and interacting with 3D worlds today means chaining external offline modules for physics, geometry, and appearance — and the seams show as physical inconsistency and visual drift. **Puffin-World** is a unified multimodal architecture that jointly models three **native world states** — **physics** (gravity field and latitude), **geometry** (depth), and **appearance** (image) — together with a unified **Omni-Camera** representation supporting diverse tasks and flexible motions. Scaling comes from **Puffin-16M**: 15M vision-language-camera triplets plus 1M trajectories with varied, challenging motions. Code, models, and datasets are released.

## What makes it unified

Two design choices carry the paper. First, physical dynamics are **propagated across future frames** with absolute camera properties grounded in the real world, so generated worlds stay physically consistent and visually stable instead of drifting frame to frame. Second, appearance and geometry are **coupled in a single generative process**: each future view is synthesized jointly with the reconstruction of its underlying geometry, rather than generating pixels now and fitting geometry later.

## Closed-loop applications

Because understanding, simulation, and generation share one paradigm, the model supports interleaved closed-loop uses — including **mimic** and **self-calibrated world exploration** — where a modular pipeline would need round-trips between separate systems. The 1M-trajectory motion split exists specifically to train these interactive behaviors under challenging camera motion.

## Relevance to SW engineers

- If you compose perception + simulation + rendering modules, audit the seams first: coupling generation with geometry reconstruction is the paper's answer to drift between stages.
- Grounding camera parameters in real-world absolutes (rather than relative/normalized values) is a cheap consistency win for any multi-view system.
- A 15M-triplet + 1M-trajectory data recipe shows the scale at which unified world models train — budget data collection accordingly.
- Released code/models/data make this directly testable before committing to the architecture.
