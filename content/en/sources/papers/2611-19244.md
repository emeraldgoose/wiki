# Agentic On-Policy Distillation Implicitly Reshapes LLMs' ReAct Patterns

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19244)
**Published**: 2026-11-14

## Overview

On-policy distillation (OPD) transfers specialized capabilities into language-model agents by supervising trajectories sampled by the student, but its cross-domain effects on interaction behavior were unclear. This paper studies how OPD reshapes ReAct (reasoning–action–observation) patterns across four domains: pure natural-language general reasoning (GR), general reasoning with code-interpreter tools (CI), ALFWorld (ALF), and τ²-bench (Barres et al., 2025), examining both single-domain OPD and multi-domain MOPD mixtures.

The headline findings are twofold. First, domain supervision shifts the route to an answer: GR supervision cuts mathematical tool-attempt rate from 70.4% to 14.4% while raising accuracy from 16.7% to 38.0%. Second, ALFWorld supervision causes severe negative transfer — τ²-bench accuracy falls from 41.5% to 6.6%, accompanied by frequent tool calls that violate user-information dependencies. To mitigate this using only source-domain training trajectories, the authors explore selective OPD guided by teacher preference changes before and after domain-specific RL; retaining the top 95% of positions raises τ²-bench accuracy from 11.5% to 29.9%, at a source-learning trade-off.

## Key points

- CI-only OPD raises ALFWorld accuracy from 0.129 to 0.402 (+0.273) and τ²-bench from 0.415 to 0.510 (+0.095); ALF-only OPD lowers τ²-bench from 0.415 to 0.066 (−0.349). Domain-specific supervision therefore transfers beyond its own task, with different outcomes per source–target pair.
- GR supervision shifts mathematics toward natural-language derivation: correct no-tool outputs rise from 2.4% (Base) to 30.1%, tool-attempt rate drops from 70.4% to 14.4%, loop-like code trajectories fall from 304 to 97, and solver calls drop from 75 to 8; accuracy rises to 38.0%. ALF supervision shows a similar shift (38.3% accuracy, 11.5% tool rate), while CI supervision keeps a 73.0% tool-attempt rate with 47.6% of its 52.4% accuracy coming from tool-containing outputs.
- CI→ALFWorld: accuracy 12.9%→40.2%, exact-repeat rate 4.5%→0.7%, post-feedback change rate 90.8%→94.3%. CI→τ²-bench: tool-attempt coverage nearly unchanged (95.8%→95.9%), retail accuracy 0.481→0.653 (55 of 76 additional successes), reference-query coverage 54.5%→67.5%, error-feedback fraction 32.6%→23.5%.
- ALF→τ²-bench failure mode: frequent "request-like" text emitted alongside tool calls that violate user-information dependencies — ask–call co-occurrence climbs steeply in the final 5% of training positions.
- Selective OPD (retaining top-Δ-token positions ranked by teacher preference change): keeping only 10% of tokens yields ~80% of the source-task (ALFWorld) gain (14.3%→69.4%) while τ²-bench only degrades to 31.1%; 10%→95% retention keeps ALF accuracy rising (69.4%→76.6%) with τ²-bench stable; the final 5% of positions drops τ²-bench from 29.9% to 11.5% and drives ask–call co-occurrence from 24.3% to 97.1%. Random 95% retention does not reproduce the Top95 result.
- MOPD (task-matched supervision mixtures) mitigates the conversational failures induced by ALF supervision on τ²-bench.

## Details

- Setup: students trained with OPD on individual domains and with MOPD on two- or three-domain mixtures; evaluated across tool-enabled/no-tool mathematics, ALFWorld, and τ²-bench at fixed source-domain validation checkpoints; large rollout batches with 50–60 updates for comparisons.
- Models/teachers: CI teacher, ALF and τ² teachers constructed per domain; students initialized from a shared base, evaluated at fixed checkpoints.
- Preference-guided selection: positions scored by the teacher's preference change (Δ) before vs. after domain-specific RL; selection retains the top retained-fraction of positions (10/20/50/95/99/100%), with GR-to-CI selective-supervision extension and GR prefix supervision as additional experiments.
- Controls: Advantage Top95/Top98 (signed ordinary-advantage exclusions, approximately coefficient-comparable), Absolute-Δ Top95 (smallest |Δ|, testing magnitude not direction), structural masking, negative masking, and ranking-control diagnostics (43.56% vs 41.02% absolute coefficient mass removed).
- Caveats: gains trade off source-domain learning; benefits are specific to the studied domains, teachers, and retention schedules; Phase 3 shows the last 5% of tokens carries the destructive interference.
