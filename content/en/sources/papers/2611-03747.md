# Enhancing Stock Market Information Retrieval with Retrieval-Augmented Generation

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03747](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03747) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Huan Phong Thai, Thu Anh Ngoc Le, Nhu Huynh Thi Le, Nhan Huu Tran, Lenh Cong Phan Pham, Hong Nhung Nguyen

## Overview

Natural language processing is widely used in financial domains, yet traditional NLP systems struggle with contextual accuracy and relevant generation in dynamic financial environments. This work presents a retrieval-augmented generation system for generating responses about stock market information. It combines retrieval-based and generative approaches to improve accuracy and relevance. Evaluation uses ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L over 100 stock market-related queries, reporting high lexical and structural similarity alongside an average response latency of 1.16 seconds. The system aims at accurate and timely financial insights rather than assuming universal market coverage.

## Key points

- RAG combines retrieval evidence with generative response production.
- The focus is stock market information retrieval and generation.
- The dataset contains 100 stock market-related queries.
- Evaluation reports ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L.
- Average response latency is 1.16 seconds.
- The results suggest timely financial insights without claiming production robustness.

## Details

The paper evaluates a generation and retrieval pipeline on a query set and latency metric. High ROUGE scores indicate alignment with reference documents but do not by themselves establish factual correctness of market claims, especially in live markets. Latency is reported at roughly 1.16 seconds on the tested setup. Practical deployment should extend the evaluation to diverse brokers, data quality issues, and user-specific intents.
