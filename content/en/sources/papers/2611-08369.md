# NPCBench: A Clinical Apprenticeship Benchmark for Multimodal Care-Pathway Reasoning in Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.08369](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08369) · **Published**: 2026-11-05
**Authors**: Pengkai Wang, Wei-Wei Zhang, Yan Li, Min Tang, Zhitian Hou, Zeyu Liu, Guanghao Zhu, Yuanyi Wang, Yanggan Gu, Wenjun Wang, Minheng Ni, Congkai Xie, Zhijie Sang, Jianmin Wu, Ying Sun, Hongxia Yang

## Overview

Current medical benchmarks often break clinical competence into isolated factual questions, single-image tasks, or static vignettes. They score individual answers but reveal less about whether a model can integrate subspecialty knowledge with multimodal evidence and track a patient's condition through care.

NPCBench introduces a clinical apprenticeship benchmark for nasopharyngeal carcinoma. It mirrors specialist training across guideline knowledge, case planning, longitudinal review, CarePath integration, and consultation. The dataset contains 4,777 component and consultation items plus a 100-patient CarePath cohort with 417 scored diagnostic and care tasks.

Episode-level evaluation tests whether diagnostic and care answers are jointly correct within a care pathway. This links stepwise tasks to patient-level trajectories, enabling assessment of evidence grounding, change interpretation, and consultation quality. Among 30 evaluated models, strong individual task performance does not ensure jointly correct diagnostic and care answers for the same patient. Strict CarePath success reaches at most 23% among 21 multimodal models.

## Key points

- Provides 4,777 component and consultation items.
- Includes a 100-patient cohort with 417 scored diagnostic and care tasks.
- Evaluates care-pathway reasoning rather than isolated medical questions.
- Tests grounding in visual evidence and longitudinal state change.
- Reveals a composition gap between task performance and care correctness.
- Achieves at most 23% strict CarePath success among multimodal models.

## Details

The paper is accepted at NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations and Datasets Track. Paired controls and physician calibration further examine evidence grounding, longitudinal revision, and consultation judging.
