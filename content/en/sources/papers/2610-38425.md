# IntReal: A Real-World Dataset and Benchmark for Multimodal Interleaved Generation

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.38425](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38425) · **Published**: 2026-10-28
**Authors**: Shaohan Yu

## Overview

Humans routinely consume multimodal content in which textual and visual information is naturally interleaved, but multimodal interleaved generation in unified multimodal models remains largely underexplored. Progress is hindered by the lack of well-defined interleaved generation tasks and corresponding training data, plus the absence of fine-grained evaluation protocols that reliably diagnose model performance across capability dimensions. IntReal addresses both with two complementary components. IntReal-100K is a real-world dataset of multimodal interleaved samples spanning four major categories. The IntReal Evaluation Suite assesses model performance across seven complementary dimensions using specialized expert models, enabling coarse-to-fine diagnosis. Extensive experiments validate the suite through low inter-metric redundancy (maximum pairwise absolute Spearman's rho at most 0.42), high agreement with human judgments (83.3% accuracy), and strong consistency across expert evaluators (Spearman's rho greater than 0.9), supporting metric complementarity and reliability. Benchmarking four families of unified multimodal models revealed distinct strengths and weaknesses across models. Post-training experiments showed the utility of IntReal-100K for improving interleaved generation and further showed that training unimodal generation can also benefit interleaved generation, suggesting that interleaved generation can benefit from modality-specific optimization rather than requiring joint text-image training at every stage.

## Key points

- IntReal-100K is a real-world interleaved multimodal dataset across four major categories.
- The evaluation suite measures seven complementary dimensions with specialized expert models.
- Maximum pairwise metric redundancy is at most 0.42 in absolute Spearman rho.
- Agreement with human judgments is 83.3%.
- Expert evaluators are consistent with Spearman rho greater than 0.9.
- Post-training showed unimodal generation training can also improve interleaved generation.

## Details

Low inter-metric redundancy and high human agreement support the evaluation suite's complementarity and reliability. Post-training results suggest modality-specific optimization can help interleaved generation without requiring joint text-image training at every stage.
