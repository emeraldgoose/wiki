# SynthFaces: Balanced Large Scale Human Dataset

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.40119](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40119) · **Published**: 2026-10-29
**Authors**: Sergey Karpukhin

## Overview

Real-world face datasets carry identity imbalance and privacy risks that bias generative models. SynthFaces proposes a controllable generation pipeline for producing a highly balanced identity-centric dataset of human faces for training and fine-tuning generative models.

The pipeline expands the Arc2Face diffusion model with a flow-matching generative model operating in the manifold of identity embeddings. This combination targets both generative fidelity and label diversity, which are difficult to achieve jointly in real human-centric datasets.

Because identities and images are synthetic, the dataset reduces reliance on real personal images. The authors empirically check identity separation with a nearest-neighbor non-leakage metric and demonstrate use in fine-tuning an adapter-based personalized generation method.

## Key points

- Introduces a pipeline for controllable, balanced large-scale face dataset generation.
- Extends Arc2Face with flow matching in identity embedding space.
- Produces the SynthFaces synthetic, identity-balanced dataset.
- Reduces direct reliance on real personal images.
- Checks identity separation with a nearest-neighbor non-leakage metric.
- Demonstrates fine-tuning of an adapter-based personalized generation method.

## Details

The motivation is to support fair and equitable AI in human-centric tasks by improving representation balance. The work is accepted at BMVC 2026 and includes code.
