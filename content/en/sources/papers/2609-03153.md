# VeriPhy: Agentic Physical Reasoning for World Model Evaluation and Refinement

Source: Wenzhuo Xu and 10 other authors, arXiv:2609.03153, Sep 2, 2026.

**Lead**: Visual fluency in generated video does not imply physical reliability — and a scalar quality score cannot say *which obligation* a clip violates or *when* it fails. **VeriPhy** is an auditable physical-verification system: a text-only planner compiles the prompt into typed physical obligations plus a statically validated execution plan *before* any frame is seen; during execution, observations only gate and scope declared calls to frozen low-level experts (segmentation/tracking, counting, eleven typed physical measurements, depth, OCR, audio-event detection). Every verdict resolves to supported / contradicted / unknown with full provenance to its evidence record. Anchored on a 1,500-clip corpus of human-annotated flaw records (space- and time-localized), VeriPhy accounts for **228 of 304** records on a 149-clip core versus **164** for a published question-decomposition evaluator on identical clips and claims.

## Obligations before observations

The key architectural discipline is ordering: obligations and the execution plan are fixed pre-observation, so the system cannot retrofit its checks to what the video happens to show. Frozen experts keep measurement stable across evaluations, and provenance-carrying evidence records make each verdict auditable one at a time — the trace doubles as the interface through which a critic verdict could be written back into generation (the "refinement" half of the title).

## Recall parity is not the story

A monolithic prompting of the same backbone reaches 222 of 304 — near VeriPhy's 228 — so raw recall alone does not separate them. What separates them is auditability: the monolith's verdicts are opaque, while every VeriPhy decision traces to typed measurements. For evaluation infrastructure, traceability beats a 6-record recall gap: you can dispute, debug, and improve a provenance chain, but not a scalar.

## Caveats

This summary is abstract-based: the obligation type system, the eleven measurements, planner validation mechanics, and cost/latency of the agentic loop are all in the full text. The 149-clip core is a subset of the 1,500-clip corpus — selection criteria matter for generalizing the 228-vs-164 gap. "Usable as the interface for write-back into generation" is a claimed affordance, not a demonstrated closed-loop result in the abstract.

## Relevance to SW engineers

- Evaluating generative video? Replace scalar quality scores with obligation-level checks localized in space and time — "what broke, when" is the actionable output.
- Fix your checks before observing outputs: pre-declared plans beat post-hoc probing for the same reason pre-registered analyses beat p-hacking.
- Freeze your measurement experts and version them — moving judges make longitudinal eval meaningless.
- Demand provenance on every automated verdict; a verdict you cannot trace is a verdict you cannot dispute or improve.
