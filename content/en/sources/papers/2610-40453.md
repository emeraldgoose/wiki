## Overview

1]School of Computer Science and Engineering, Beihang University 2]MT Lab, Meitu Inc. 3]Department of Automation, Tsinghua University \checkdata[Project Page]https://arthuring.github.io/PhysInsert/ We present PhysInsert, a framework for physically plausible reference-based object insertion that jointly adapts objects and their surrounding scenes. Existing methods often rely on masked inputs, generation-based pairs, or training pairs with limited viewpoint variation, leaving pose and illumination adaptation and bidirectional object–scene interactions insufficiently constrained. We construct the Insert World dataset with strictly aligned Blender-rendered counterfactual pairs and object references across viewpoints and lighting conditions.
