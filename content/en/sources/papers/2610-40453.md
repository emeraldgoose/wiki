# PhysInsert: Physically Plausible Object Insertion with Geometric Priors and Bidirectional Object-Scene Interactions

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.40453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40453) · **Published**: 2026-10-29
**Authors**: Yue Gong

## Overview

Existing reference-based object insertion methods often rely on masked inputs, generation-based pairs, or training pairs with limited viewpoint variation, leaving pose and illumination adaptation and bidirectional object-scene interactions insufficiently constrained. PhysInsert is a framework for physically plausible reference-based object insertion that jointly adapts objects and their surrounding scenes. It constructs the Insert World dataset with strictly aligned Blender-rendered counterfactual pairs and object references across viewpoints and lighting conditions. Building on these data, Geometry-Guided Adaptation couples a VGGT geometry stream with image and text streams under target-scene feature supervision to guide spatially consistent insertion. Complementary 2D Object-Scene Spatial Supervision jointly supervises object occupancy and environmental changes while grounding spatial instructions in object regions through image-text attention. PhysInsert-Bench introduces dedicated cross-environment and cross-view cases requiring illumination and pose adaptation beyond appearance copying. Experiments on PhysInsert-Bench and DreamBooth demonstrate improved image fidelity, and a user study further shows more accurate placement and improved lighting integration, perspective, and pose plausibility.

## Key points

- Reference-based insertion usually lacks pose and illumination adaptation.
- Insert World provides aligned counterfactual pairs across viewpoints and lighting.
- Geometry-Guided Adaptation couples geometry with image and text streams.
- Complementary supervision jointly supervises occupancy and environmental changes.
- PhysInsert-Bench requires cross-environment and cross-view adaptation.
- PhysInsert improves image fidelity on benchmarks.
- User studies favor its placement, lighting, perspective, and pose plausibility.

## Details

The framework jointly adapts object and scene rather than copying appearance. Geometry-guided adaptation and spatial supervision constrain interactions so that object insertion affects and is affected by the environment.
