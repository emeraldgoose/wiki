# MPEG-Diffusion: Mixed-Resolution Video Generation

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12069)
**Published**: 2026-11-09

## Overview

Video diffusion transformers (DiTs) produce high-quality video, but attention cost grows quadratically with token count as resolution and duration increase. Mixed-resolution generation — high resolution (HR) for the visually important region, low resolution (LR) elsewhere — is a natural fix, yet prior methods suffer severe quality drops on video and need the HR region specified in advance. MPEG-Diffusion argues these failures are not inherent to mixed resolution and addresses three root causes: (1) the curse of mixed resolution, where HR and LR tokens interfere in attention — solved with dual-resolution attention, in which each query reads the K/V of its own resolution; (2) distortion at the HR/LR boundary — solved with a mixed-resolution decoder that blends HR and LR latents before decoding; and (3) needing the HR region in advance — solved with a self-selected HR region predicted by the DiT itself.

Processing only 30% of full-resolution tokens, MPEG-Diffusion speeds up denoising by 4.8x on Wan2.1 14B (3.8x on 1.3B), outperforming prior token-matched mixed-resolution and token-merging baselines on nearly every quality metric. In a human study of 400 video pairs, raters preferred MPEG-Diffusion over prior mixed-resolution methods in over 90% of pairs, rating it close to full resolution.

## Key points

- Setup: Wan2.1 1.3B at 480p and 14B at 720p (704x1280, one LR token per 2x2 HR tokens), 81 frames, 50 denoising steps; full fine-tuning on 64 NVIDIA B200 GPUs (1,250 steps DiT, 4k steps mixer/upscaler, ~2,500 labeled clips for the region head). Images evaluated on FLUX.2 [klein] 4B at 1024x1024.
- Baselines: ToMe, IDM (token merging), Phase-Aligned RoPE and Foveated Diffusion (prior mixed-resolution), with a moving circular HR region since gaze is unavailable in text-to-video.
- At k=40 (30% tokens), 14B: 4.8x speedup, MANIQA 0.397, DOVER 0.794, VBench Quality 83.9 — best quality among token-matched methods and within 0.025 DOVER of HR (HR: 0.819, 85.3 VBench).
- At k=40 (30% tokens), 1.3B: 3.8x speedup, MANIQA 0.345, DOVER 0.762 (HR: 0.778); prior methods collapse toward noise on detail-sensitive metrics (ToMe DOVER 0.119, IDM 0.110).
- At k=25 (38% tokens), 14B: 3.3x speedup, MANIQA 0.402, DOVER 0.803, VBench 84.1 — again best in class.
- Human study (400 MovieGen pairs): raters prefer MPEG-Diffusion over prior mixed-resolution methods in >90% of pairs, close to HR quality.
- First k of T steps run at LR; at step k a linear head selects the HR region M from the DiT's own state, and the remaining steps denoise M at HR while the rest stay at LR.
- Late-step self-selection needs no gaze annotation or predefined region path, unlike foveated approaches.

## Details

- Metrics: MANIQA, imaging quality, DOVER on 1,003 MovieGen prompts; VBench Quality on 944 VBench prompts; human study on 400 MovieGen pairs; images: Q-Align and patch FID on 1,000 held-out prompts plus a GPT-5.6 pairwise test.
- Speed is denoising time on one NVIDIA B200 relative to HR; HR and LR are references, not competitors.
- On VBench Quality the method is statistically close to LR/HR (the metric is blur-insensitive); the advantage shows on detail-sensitive metrics (MANIQA, DOVER) and human ratings.
- MPEG is a backronym: mixed-resolution generation in the spirit of perceptual coding — spend bits (tokens) where the eye looks.
