# SeMo3D: Unifying 3D Generation, Editing, and Completion via Selective Modeling

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.02568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02568) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Xiaokun Sun, Jian Yang, Zhenyu Zhang

## Overview

3D generation, editing, and completion are generally treated as regenerating the complete 3D asset under task-specific constraints, even when only a local region needs modification. This leads to task isolation, inconsistency in content that should remain unchanged, and dependence on paired data. SeMo3D is based on the observation that all three tasks synthesize a designated region while keeping the rest intact: the full region for generation, the target region for editing, and the missing region for completion. This paradigm, termed Selective Modeling, preserves unchanged content by construction and allows training without paired editing or completion data because any 3D asset can be partitioned into a selected region and its context.

## Key points

- Generation, editing, and completion are unified under one selective paradigm.
- Only the designated region is synthesized; unchanged content is preserved.
- Training can avoid task-specific paired editing and completion data.
- Region-selective denoising applies noise and loss only to random regions.
- Omni3DiT handles image, text, and point guidance with region distinction.
- SeMo3D surpasses state-of-the-art methods on three benchmarks.

## Details

SeMo3D has two main technical components. Region-selective denoising randomly selects regions of varying sizes on standard 3D assets and applies noise and loss only to them, jointly learning all three tasks. Omni3DiT is a multi-condition 3D diffusion transformer that distinguishes regions to create from those to retain and fuses image, text, and point guidance for fine-grained control. Experiments across three benchmarks report multimodal controllability in generation and strong consistency and accuracy in editing and completion. The paper also positions SeMo3D as a unified modeling tool for agents and natural-language-driven 3D creation, though general deployment and asset-size limits are not the main focus.
