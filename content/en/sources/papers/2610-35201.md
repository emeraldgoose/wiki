# DTCA: Denoising Token Credit Assignment for Reinforcement Learning in Diffusion Language Models

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.35201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35201) · **Published**: 2026-10-26
**Authors**: Hongze Chen, Miao Pan, Lizhou Liang, Qinfeng Li, Xinyin Ma, Yongliang Shen, Weishi Wang, Daniel Dahlmeier, Xuhong Zhang, Wenqi Zhang

## Overview

Diffusion language models (DLMs) generate text through iterative denoising, enabling bidirectional context modeling and parallel token refinement. Recent studies have applied reinforcement learning (RL) to DLMs and improved reasoning performance, but iterative denoising makes credit assignment hard: final outcome rewards give limited information about how different tokens contribute.

DTCA estimates token contributions by measuring the improvement in prediction confidence of remaining masked positions after a token is committed, grouping these signals by part-of-speech (POS) categories, and calibrating them by token-level entropy changes induced by policy updates.

## Key points

- Proposes DTCA, a token-level credit assignment framework for RL in diffusion language models.
- Estimates token contribution via confidence improvement on remaining masked positions after commitment.
- Groups contribution signals by POS categories to select informative denoising timesteps.
- Calibrates learning signals using token-level entropy changes from policy updates.
- On LLaDA-8B-Instruct: up to +9.0 on MATH500 and +5.3 on GSM8K across generation budgets.

## Details

The method addresses sparse final-reward credit by attributing improvement at the token/denoising step. POS grouping and entropy calibration make the attributed signals more stable for RL updates.
