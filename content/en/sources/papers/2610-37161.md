# WorkFactory: Turning Professional Intents into Executable Work for Scalable Agent Training

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.37161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37161) · **Published**: 2026-10-27
**Authors**: Ziyang Zeng, Tingyang Chen, Jia Deng, Qiyao Wang, Shuo Tang, Yike Yang, Tao Ren, Wayne Xin Zhao, Feng Chang, Jujie He, Chuan Hao, Ran Tao, Bryan Dai

## Overview

Scaling supervised training for working agents requires diverse professional tasks that yield reliable expert demonstrations, but missing or inconsistent task information can obstruct teacher execution and weaken supervision. WorkFactory is a scalable framework for constructing coherent and executable professional tasks. Starting from professional intents, information-level planning builds an information support graph to guide task instantiation, explicitly representing dependencies and locations of information required for intended outputs. Probe executions then identify support failures and guide repairs while preserving the intended work. The framework constructed 112,686 tasks spanning 612 occupations, and the collected teacher trajectories were used to fine-tune Qwen models at 4B, 27B, and 35B-A3B scales, producing the WorkPilot family. All three models improved over their base models across four professional-work benchmarks, with WorkPilot-35B-A3B rising from 879 to 1565 Elo on GDPval-AA v2.1. Scaling analyses revealed model-dependent diminishing returns, with execution length first increasing and then decreasing. Controlled component analyses showed that planning and repair reduced probe-diagnosed task-environment defects and improved downstream performance. Data and models are available.

## Key points

- Information-level planning builds an information support graph from professional intents.
- Probe executions identify support failures and guide repair while preserving intended work.
- The framework produced 112,686 tasks across 612 occupations.
- WorkPilot models were trained at 4B, 27B, and 35B-A3B scales.
- All three improved over base models on four professional-work benchmarks.
- WorkPilot-35B-A3B rose from 879 to 1565 Elo on GDPval-AA v2.1.

## Details

Planning and repair tackle defects in the task environment rather than the model: information support graphs make dependencies explicit, and probe-guided repair preserves work intent. Scaling analyses indicate diminishing, model-dependent returns and non-monotonic execution-length behavior.
