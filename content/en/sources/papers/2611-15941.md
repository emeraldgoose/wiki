# Lang-SVG: Hierarchical Image Vectorization with Language Priors

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.15941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15941) · **Published**: 2026-11-11

## Overview

Image vectorization reconstructs raster images as compact scalable vector graphics (SVG), providing a structured and editable code representation of images. However, most existing methods optimize for pixel-level fidelity, often producing SVGs that lack alignment with human-perceived object-part hierarchies, making them difficult to manipulate.

To address this, we propose Lang-SVG, which introduces an explicit tree structure to organize SVG primitives with semantic labels and parent-child relationships. Lang-SVG incorporates granularity-controllable and open-vocabulary segmentation priors to initialize object-part hierarchies, and leverages multimodal large language models (MLLMs) with SVG tree context to assign semantic labels to unlabeled nodes.

To reduce redundancy, we introduce depth-adaptive simplification to reduce shape complexity and Tree-Guided Pruning and Merging to eliminate redundant SVG nodes. L1-SAM-Prompting further recovers missing regions to improve reconstruction fidelity and structural completeness.

Beyond reconstruction fidelity, we introduce a new evaluation protocol that measures structural quality and semantic part alignment. Experiments demonstrate that Lang-SVG achieves state-of-the-art performance in both rendering fidelity and structural quality.
