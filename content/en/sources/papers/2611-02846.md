# SpecOPD: Branch-Parallel On-Policy Distillation for Speculative Decoding

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.02846](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02846) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Tianyu Liu, Jun Zhang, Wenjie Huang, Yuhao Shen, Tianqi Xu, Xinyue Hao, Hongyaoxing Gu, Xuyang Liu, Baolin Zhang, Yating Zhu, Shuang Ge, Jun Dai, Lei Chen, Yue Li, Mingchen Wan

## Overview

Speculative decoding accelerates LLM inference by reducing the sequential cost of token-by-token generation. Advanced methods reduce proposal cost with parallel drafters that predict entire blocks in one forward pass, but overall speedup still depends on acceptance length. On-policy distillation can push well-trained drafters beyond acceptance-length plateaus, yet conventional pipelines collect supervision through sequential draft-and-verify rollouts. The key insight is that on-policy supervision need not inherit these dependencies: a draft block can be supervised using only its reference prefix and draft-sampled history. SpecOPD, a branch-parallel on-policy distillation framework, parallelizes draft sampling and target supervision across independent branches drawn from the evolving drafter policy at reference anchors. Branches are packed into proposal trees to share target-prefix computation, with tree masks for full attention, branch-consistent indexer groups for sparse attention, and indexed state replay for linear attention to preserve each branch's history. The drafter is trained with an E2E-TV surrogate that directly targets acceptance length. The study spans 8B full-attention, 27B full-linear, and 125B sparse-linear targets, three parallel draft architectures (DFlash, DSpark, DFlash V2), and four task domains (dialogue, coding, mathematics, agentic). SpecOPD achieves up to 41.45% higher mean acceptance length, 16.09x supervised-token throughput over sequential-rollout OPD under native configurations, and up to 3.56x generation speedup over autoregressive decoding.

## Key points

- Speculative decoding gains depend on acceptance length even with parallel block-proposal drafters.
- On-policy supervision can be collected without strict draft-and-verify sequential dependencies.
- SpecOPD samples and supervises independent branches at reference anchors and packs them into proposal trees.
- Attention and state replay techniques preserve branch history while sharing target-prefix computation.
- An E2E-TV surrogate directly optimizes acceptance length; accuracy is up to +41.45% acceptance, 16.09x OPD throughput, and 3.56x autoregressive speedup.

## Details

Evaluation covers 8B full-attention, 27B full-linear, and 125B sparse-linear targets, three draft architectures (DFlash, DSpark, DFlash V2), and dialogue, coding, mathematics, and agentic domains. Configuration details and full speedup tables are in the linked source.
