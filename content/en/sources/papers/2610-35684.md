# OmniShowcase: Omnidirectional Subject-Consistent Video Generation from Multi-View Images

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.35684](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35684) · **Published**: 2026-10-26
**Authors**: Junyeong Ahn, Geonwoo Kim, Kinam Kim, Gihwi Kim, Hoonjin Jung, Hyojin Jang, Jaegul Choo

## Overview

Generating videos of a specific subject from a few reference images remains challenging when the camera reveals surfaces not visible in the input. Early subject-to-video (S2V) methods condition on a single reference image and must infer unseen appearance and geometry. Recent multi-view S2V methods provide more information but do not explicitly specify the 3D structure connecting observations, and training data may not preserve the same subject under viewpoint changes.

OmniShowcase combines an automated data synthesis pipeline along diverse camera trajectories guided by subject geometry, including full 360-degree orbits, with geometry-conditioning via a turntable of surface-normal maps reconstructed from reference views.

## Key points

- Introduces OmniShowcase for omnidirectional subject-consistent video generation from multi-view images.
- Synthesizes training videos along diverse trajectories with geometry-driven viewpoint variation, including 360-degree orbits.
- Conditions the video model on a turntable of surface-normal maps reconstructed from reference views, without temporal alignment to generated frames.
- Releases Showcase-4K (3D-asset videos with 12 camera motions over 88 trajectory configurations) and ShowcaseBench (held-out set with source meshes and camera parameters).
- Achieves state-of-the-art subject identity and 3D consistency while maintaining video quality and motion.

## Details

By making underlying 3D geometry explicit through surface-normal turntables and diverse geometry-driven orbits, the method addresses the mismatch between multi-view references and consistent subject rendering.
