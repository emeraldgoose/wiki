# EPSBench: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Earnings Forecasting

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.38293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38293) · **Published**: 2026-10-28
**Authors**: Liqin Ye

## Overview

Quarterly earnings forecasting requires an agent to connect financial evidence to the income-statement line items that produce a company's reported earnings. EPSBench studies this ability across 149 firm-quarters from 86 US companies. Agents read a fixed collection of filings, earnings-call transcripts, and news, then estimate company-specific income-statement drivers with cited evidence. A shared accounting calculator converts these estimates into GAAP diluted earnings per share, providing a common basis for comparing forecast assumptions. Six models were evaluated under a common harness. Their point forecast errors were assessed relative to date-matched analyst consensus, and whether prediction intervals achieved stated coverage was checked. Three models showed no detectable paired difference in point forecast error from consensus, while three had higher error. Across all six models, nominal 80% intervals contained reported EPS in only 37-70% of runs that returned an interval, and most misses fell above the upper bound because forecasts ran low. Driver records trace most of the error gap between models to operating-income estimates.

## Key points

- The benchmark covers 149 firm-quarters from 86 US companies.
- Agents estimate income-statement drivers with cited evidence.
- A shared accounting calculator converts estimates into GAAP diluted EPS.
- Three of six models were statistically tied with consensus in point forecast error.
- Three models had higher error than consensus.
- Nominal 80% intervals contained reported EPS in only 37-70% of runs, mostly due to low forecasts.

## Details

Operating-income estimates account for most of the inter-model error gap according to driver records. The benchmark separates forecast point error, interval coverage, and cited evidence, enabling diagnosis of where assumptions fail rather than only final EPS error.
