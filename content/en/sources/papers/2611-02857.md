# Solv3R: 3D Looped Transformer as a Multi-view Solver

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.02857](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02857) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Jeong-gi Kwak, Andrea Tagliasacchi, Sho Kagami, Yuki Ono, Kwang Moo Yi

## Overview

Classical structure-from-motion reconstruction proceeds iteratively, refining camera poses and scene geometry from a current estimate, which allows maps to expand without recomputing already completed views or to improve geometry as more observations are provided. Most geometric foundation models, however, predict entire geometry in a single forward pass or update a recurrent state as frames arrive sequentially, making expansion and refinement non-trivial. The paper introduces Solv3R, a looped transformer that learns to continue solving multi-view geometry from its current state. Importantly, its update operator is independent of the iteration count, allowing previously refined and newly introduced views to be jointly updated regardless of how "solved" they are. The authors train the operator to preserve accurate reconstructions under continued iteration and to correct inaccurate states, enabling refinement of fixed image collections and integration of new observations. Representations of solved views can also be cached once and reused as geometric context across iterations, enabling compact and efficient key-value caching. The solver dynamics are analyzed and compared with state-of-the-art methods, showing that a single learned solver supports offline reconstruction, incremental reconstruction, relocalization, and streaming without task-specific training or modules.

## Key points

- Classical SfM refines geometry iteratively, while many foundation models predict in one pass or update sequentially.
- Solv3R is a looped transformer whose count-independent update operator jointly refines old and new views.
- Training preserves reconstruction under iteration and corrects inaccurate states for fixed collections or new observations.
- Solved view representations can be cached and reused as geometric context for efficient KV caching.
- One learned solver supports offline, incremental, relocalization, and streaming use without task-specific modules.

## Details

The paper analyzes solver dynamics and compares with prior methods. Offline and incremental scenarios are covered in the experiments. Code and models are to be released; implementation specifics are in the linked source.
