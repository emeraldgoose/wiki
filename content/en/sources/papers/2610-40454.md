# Glance: The Generator Is Its Own Reranker

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.40454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40454) · **Published**: 2026-10-29
**Authors**: Adilet Metinov

## Overview

Retrieval-augmented generation commonly serves separate models for reranking and answering, duplicating model residency and passage processing. Glance equips the generator with a lightweight decision adapter and pointer head to score candidate passages from a shared prefill. Short relevance probes branch from the cached context, producing one score per passage without separately prefilling each query-passage pair. A single resident 9B backbone supports generation, general decisions, and reranking, with approximately 0.1 GiB of additional memory per decision skill. After 39 minutes of MS MARCO fine-tuning, Glance achieved nDCG@10 of 0.741 on SciFact and 0.345 on NFCorpus, compared with 0.721 and 0.326 for bge-reranker-v2-m3. On 300 HotpotQA questions, its selected context improved answer F1 by 0.045 over cross-encoder-selected context under the same base generator (paired bootstrap 95% CI [0.014, 0.076]). In a scaling study, reranking quality kept rising up to 200 candidates (43k tokens) scored from one shared prefill, where it exceeded the cross-encoder by 0.058 nDCG@10, and a probe-only activated adapter lets generation continue from the original passage cache. These initial results demonstrate generator-based reranking with one resident backbone; evaluation currently covers two retrieval datasets, one QA dataset, and one backbone family.

## Key points

- RAG often duplicates residency and prefill by using separate ranker and generator models.
- Glance adds a decision adapter and pointer head to the generator.
- Scores come from a shared prefill via short relevance probes.
- A 9B backbone supports generation and reranking with about 0.1 GiB extra per skill.
- Glance reached 0.741 SciFact and 0.345 NFCorpus nDCG@10.
- It beat bge-reranker-v2-m3 on both datasets.
- Scaling to 200 candidates exceeded the cross-encoder by 0.058 nDCG@10.

## Details

The scoring path avoids prefilling every query-passage pair separately. A probe-only activated adapter allows generation to continue from the original passage cache after reranking, supporting a single resident model setup.
