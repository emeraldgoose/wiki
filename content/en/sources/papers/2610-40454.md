# Glance: The Generator Is Its Own Reranker

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40454)
**Date**: 2026-10-29


## Overview

Retrieval-augmented generation commonly serves separate models for reranking and answering, duplicating model residency and passage processing. We introduce Glance, which equips the generator with a lightweight decision adapter and pointer head to score candidate passages from a shared prefill. Short relevance probes branch from the cached context, producing one score per passage without separately prefilling each query–passage pair. A single resident 9B backbone supports generation, general decisions and reranking, with approximately 0.1 GiB of additional memory per decision skill. After 39 m RAG systems often assign evidence selection and answer generation to separate models.
