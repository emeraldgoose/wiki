Coding agents are increasingly evolving beyond reproducing what a world looks like toward constructing executable worlds that capture how a world works. This shift calls for executable world modeling, which integrates two core capabilities: world understanding, reasoning about the underlying structural, mechanical, and causal principles of a world, and executable realization, translating that understanding into a code-based system whose behavior emerges from physical interactions.
