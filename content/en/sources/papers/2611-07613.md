# Real-Time Full-Waveform LiDAR Transformers via Intensity-Guided Token Reduction and Physics-Aware Augmentation

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.07613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07613) · **Published**: 2026-11-05
**Authors**: Kotaro Oishi, Kazuma Ikeda, Ryosei Hara, Ryo Yoshida, Mariko Isogawa, Kentaro Yoshioka

## Overview

LiDAR is critical for autonomous driving and robotics, but it remains vulnerable to fog, rain, transparent objects, and low-reflectance surfaces. Full-waveform LiDAR captures the complete return waveform as a histogram, preserving rich reflection characteristics such as multipath echoes and weak signals.

Transformer-based FWL methods face two bottlenecks: high computational cost from redundant tokens in low-intensity background regions, and limited generalization due to scarce annotated data. The paper addresses both by exploiting FWL's physical structure. FWL-ToPM selectively reduces tokens using intensity distributions while retaining critical waveform content. FWLAug provides physics-aware augmentation preserving time-of-flight structure and frustum geometry.

On the Ghost-FWL benchmark, FWL-ToPM achieves over eight times speedup at real-time throughput with nearly nine points F1 gain over the prior state of the art, establishing the first real-time FWL Transformer with simultaneous accuracy improvement. FWLAug further improves standard and out-of-distribution performance. The code and dataset are publicly available.

## Key points

- Targets fog, rain, transparent objects, and low-reflectance surfaces with full-waveform LiDAR.
- Addresses computational cost from redundant background tokens.
- Addresses generalization from scarce annotated FWL data.
- Introduces FWL-ToPM for intensity-guided token pruning and merging.
- Introduces FWLAug for physics-aware augmentation.
- Achieves real-time speedup and F1 improvement on Ghost-FWL.
