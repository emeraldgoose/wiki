# AnchorComplete: Recovering Complete Scene Meshes from Partial Point Clouds

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03228](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03228) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Weirong Chen, Ismail El-Yamany, Ganlin Zhang, Chuanxia Zheng, Andrea Vedaldi, Daniel Cremers

## Overview

</span>Most image-based 3D reconstructors primarily recover visible surfaces, producing partial point clouds with large holes and leaving unobserved geometry unresolved. To address this limitation, we introduce AnchorComplete, a framework that reconstructs complete scene meshes from partial point clouds while preserving observed geometry and supporting scenes of varying scales. Building on TRELLIS.2, our method combines partial-point conditioning, which integrates global scene tokens with local spatial evidence to anchor generation to the input, and metric-scale coarse-to-fine generation, which progressively refines scene geometry within fixed-size windows while maintaining global structural consistency. Experiments on Replica and SCRREAM demonstrate that AnchorComplete consistently outperforms strong baselines in complete-scene reconstruction, particularly under sparse-view settings. </blockquote> <!--CONTEXT--> <div class="metatable"> <table summary="Additional metadata"> <tr> <td class="tablecell label">Comments:</td> <td class="tablecell comments mathjax">Project page: <a href="https://wrchen530.github.io/anchor-complete" rel="external noopener nofollow" class="link-external link-https">this https URL</a></td> </tr> <tr> <td class="tablecell label">
