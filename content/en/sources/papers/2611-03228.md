# AnchorComplete: Recovering Complete Scene Meshes from Partial Point Clouds

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03228](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03228) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Weirong Chen, Ismail El-Yamany, Ganlin Zhang, Chuanxia Zheng, Andrea Vedaldi, Daniel Cremers

## Overview

Most image-based 3D reconstructors primarily recover visible surfaces, producing partial point clouds with large holes and leaving unobserved geometry unresolved. The paper introduces AnchorComplete, a framework that reconstructs complete scene meshes from partial point clouds while preserving observed geometry and supporting scenes of varying scales. Building on TRELLIS.2, the method combines partial-point conditioning and metric-scale coarse-to-fine generation. Partial-point conditioning integrates global scene tokens with local spatial evidence to anchor generation to the input. Metric-scale coarse-to-fine generation progressively refines scene geometry within fixed-size windows while maintaining global structural consistency. Experiments on Replica and SCRREAM demonstrate that AnchorComplete consistently outperforms strong baselines in complete-scene reconstruction, particularly under sparse-view settings.

## Key points

- Image-based reconstruction usually recovers visible surfaces, leaving large unobserved holes.
- AnchorComplete reconstructs complete scene meshes from partial point clouds while preserving observed geometry and scale flexibility.
- It builds on TRELLIS.2 with partial-point conditioning and metric-scale coarse-to-fine generation.
- Partial-point conditioning anchors generation to input using global scene tokens plus local spatial evidence.
- Reproduction on Replica and SCRREAM shows consistent gains over strong baselines, especially in sparse-view settings.
