# Democratizing Robot-Use Agents: A Unified Harness with Open Models

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.01560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01560) · **Published**: 2026-11-01
**Authors**: Jiarui Yuan, Yuxin Zuo, Xiaoyu Huang, Wenbo Zhang, Kwanyoung Park, Junlin Yang, Tianxiang Zhang, Ruoqu Chen, Yutao Ouyang, Bingxiang He, Xingtai Lv, Hongjin Wu, Jiajun Liu, Jiahao Zhao, Tianwei Luo, Haozhan Li, Cheng Qian, Jiaxin Ge, Huan-ang Gao, Kaisen Yang, Jiaqi Deng, Qixin Xu, Siqiao Huang, Bowen Zhou, Mengdi Xu, Zhiyuan Liu, Pieter Abbeel, Ning Ding

## Overview

Robot-use agents have shown promising zero-shot manipulation capabilities, but reliance on frontier proprietary models limits research into further training. uni-robot-agent is introduced as a unified harness for competitive open-weight robot use that supports further training. Without privileged inputs, an out-of-the-box open model achieves a RoboDojo Score of 31.39, surpassing the published GPT-class result of 28.97 and many VLA/WAM baselines, while remaining competitive on 19 real-world tasks. The harness democratizes robot-use agent research by removing dependence on closed models.

## Key points

- Robot-use agents can manipulate objects with zero-shot behavior.
- Frontier proprietary models limit open research.
- uni-robot-agent provides an open-weight alternative.
- The harness supports further training.
- An open model reaches a competitive RoboDojo Score.
- It performs competitively on many real-world tasks.

## Details

The contribution is infrastructural as much as methodological. By unifying the agent harness and supporting open models, the project enables researchers to study training improvements instead of only calling closed APIs. The reported benchmark results show that open-weight models can be competitive with proprietary baselines on robot manipulation tasks. The work emphasizes democratization of agent research and invites further training experiments on accessible models.
