# Holo4D: Holistic 4D Reconstruction as Geometric Control for Video Diffusion

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.25276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25276) · **Published**: 2026-10-18
**Authors**: Yushi Lan, Zeren Jiang, Koichi Namekata, Xingang Pan, Chuanxia Zheng, Andrea Vedaldi

## Overview

Synthesizing a monocular video from a new camera trajectory requires preserving observed scene geometry and motion while completing regions that become newly visible. Feed-forward 4D reconstructors recover the structure of the observed scene, whereas video diffusion models provide a generative prior for completing unseen content. Holo4D is a video reshooting framework that uses reconstructed 3D trajectories as a holistic source-view 4D scaffold. It projects these trajectories into requested views and encodes their geometry and source appearance to guide a pretrained video diffusion model. Together with the source video and target cameras, the scaffold grounds generation in reconstructed observations while leaving unsupported regions to the generative prior. Experiments demonstrate the value of trajectory-based conditioning for camera control and novel-view synthesis. The framework accommodates different 4D reconstructors without retraining the video generator, allowing it to benefit from future advances in source reconstruction.

## Key points

- Feed-forward 4D reconstruction preserves observed geometry and motion.
- Video diffusion models complete unseen content with a generative prior.
- Holo4D projects reconstructed 3D trajectories into target views as a scaffold.
- The scaffold grounds generation while unsupported regions use the generative prior.
- The framework works with different 4D reconstructors without retraining the diffusion model.

## Details

The method separates source reconstruction from generation. The 4D scaffold encodes geometry and source appearance and, alongside the source video and target cameras, guides a pretrained diffusion model. This decoupling means improvements in source reconstruction can be adopted without retraining the generator.
