# Holo4D: Holistic 4D Reconstruction as Geometric Control for Video Diffusion

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25276)
**Date**: 2026-10-18


## Overview

Synthesizing a monocular video from a new camera trajectory requires preserving the observed scene geometry and motion while completing regions that become newly visible. Feed-forward 4D reconstructors recover the structure of the observed scene, whereas video diffusion models provide a generative prior for completing unseen content. We introduce Holo4D, a video reshooting framework that uses reconstructed 3D trajectories as a holistic source-view 4D scaffold.
