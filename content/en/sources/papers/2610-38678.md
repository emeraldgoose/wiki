## Overview

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) have made significant progress in video understanding and reasoning in recent years (Kimi Team, 2026; Qwen Team, 2026; Google DeepMind, 2026; OpenAI, 2026). Beyond advancements in model architecture and training paradigms, research on interaction paradigms is equally important for advancing models toward more adaptive and generalizable intelligence in real-world environments.
