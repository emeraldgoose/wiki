# After One-Click Video: The Inflow of Service-Type Content, the Failure of the Recommendation Pool Mechanism, and the BoLe System

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37695)
**Date**: 2026-10-24


## Overview

The Inflow of Service-Type Content, the Failure of the Recommendation Pool Mechanism, and the BoLe System In September 2026, a new large language model was released. Soon after, a new way of making explainer videos became very popular: people asked the model to write a program, and the program produced the video. “One-click video” content then appeared in large amounts on many platforms. On the surface, this is a rise in creative power at the level of the individual. We argue that, at a deeper level, it changes the structure of the content industry. This judgment rests on two observations. First, this wave of content is service-type content. People watch it to meet a need, not to enjoy the content
