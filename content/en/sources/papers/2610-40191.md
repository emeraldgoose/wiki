# Video Generation Meets Agent Harness: Agentic Video Generation, Agentic World Modeling and Recursive Self-Improvement

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.40191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40191) · **Published**: 2026-10-29
**Authors**: Liaoyuan Fan, Yuhao Zheng, Dongxing Mao, Hangyu Ran, Siyuan Hu, Weijia Mao, Rui Zhao, Weijia Wu, Guian Fang, Yanzhe Chen, Zeyu Zhu, Yuxin Jiang, Yiren Song, Yuchao Gu, Junchao Yi, Qi Hu, Dan Kong, Alex Jinpeng Wang, Kevin Qinghong Lin, Mike Zheng Shou, Wei Liu

## Overview

Video generation is evolving from end-to-end synthesis toward agentic generation, where planning, tool use, and feedback produce coherent multi-shot videos with greater control. At the same time, video world models provide action-conditioned observations and predicted futures, serving as interactive environments for agents. The paper studies the intersection of these areas through the agent harness: the executable runtime that constructs an agent's context, exposes tools, maintains state, routes feedback, and enforces budgets around models such as video generators.

The report is organized around three directions: agentic video generation, agentic world modeling, and recursive self-improvement. It frames the harness as the layer that makes advanced video models usable by agents, rather than treating generation or world modeling as isolated capabilities.

## Key points

- Video generation is moving from direct synthesis toward planned, tool-using agentic pipelines.
- Multi-shot coherence depends on feedback and control, not only model capacity.
- Video world models can provide action-conditioned futures for agents.
- An agent harness manages context, tools, state, feedback, and budgets.
- The report organizes the field around three directions.
- The harness is presented as the integration layer between models and agents.

## Details

The paper treats video models as components within a larger agent system. Generation alone does not manage task state, tool access, or budget constraints, so an executable runtime must orchestrate these resources. World models add predictive environments that agents can interact with, while recursive self-improvement suggests using generated experience to refine the same pipeline. The result is a systems-level view of how video generation and agent infrastructure can co-evolve.
