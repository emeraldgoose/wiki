# Venus-DefakerOne2: A Vision-Forensics Foundation Model with Broader Coverage

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.37918](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37918) · **Published**: 2026-10-27
**Authors**: Venus-DefakerOne2 team (inclusionAI)

## Overview

Real-world forgery is heterogeneous in both form and media: digital manipulation and physical spoofing appear across images and videos. Existing detectors are often fragmented, specialized to one manipulation family, format, or acquisition setting. Venus-DefakerOne2 proposes a unified 2B-parameter vision-forensics foundation model for the full {digital, physical} x {image, video} matrix.

It trains an MLLM on roughly 50M anti-forgery examples from public datasets, self-constructed data, and manually annotated online attacks. To reconcile efficiency, generalization, and fitting capacity it uses Tide-Style Tuning, three alternating phases analogous to tidal flow. To handle the modality dilemma of video forensics, it uses Modality Synergism, aligning native video clips with extracted frame sequences during training and inference.

## Key points

- Targets the full matrix of digital/physical forgery in images and videos with one model.
- Uses Qwen3.5-2B as the MLLM backbone for implicit semantic reasoning and video understanding.
- Adds an Anomaly Magnifier module to strengthen fine-grained forensic cue perception.
- Tide-Style Tuning uses LoRA adaptation, full training, and freezing-based specialization in alternating phases.
- Modality Synergism addresses video evidence distributed unevenly across frames.
- Evaluated on 53 forgery detection and 14 localization benchmarks, ranking first across eight domains.
- Reports averages improving from 80.4 to 90.9 on images and 57.6 to 95.7 on videos over the predecessor.
- Introduces RRVBench, generated from recent closed-source AIGC video generators, for realistic video rumor detection.

## Details

The authors argue that a practical system must handle all combinations of forgery form and media rather than one scenario. Instead of choosing between temporal and spatial processing, Modality Synergism processes each video both as a native clip and as a frame sequence with adaptive frame rate. Tide-Style Tuning phases aim to balance generalization through lightweight adaptation, capacity through full training, and specialization through freezing.

Experiments span many benchmarks and both detection and localization tasks. The model is intended to reduce deployment complexity by replacing scenario-specific detectors with a single foundation model.
