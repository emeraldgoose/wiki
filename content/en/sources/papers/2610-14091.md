## Overview

Training on broad, unfiltered data enables high-quality image generation, but can also teach models concepts that should not be generated; restricting the training data ensures safety, but reduces quality because examples containing unsafe concepts also provide useful knowledge that is not itself unsafe. Existing approaches largely address this trade-off post hoc through concept unlearning, which can lead to catastrophic forgetting as the number of concepts to remove grows.
