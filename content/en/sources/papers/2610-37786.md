# WebAnything: From the Wild Web to Scalable Agent Environments

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.37786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37786) · **Published**: 2026-10-27
**Authors**: Tianci Xie

## Overview

Web agents require diverse, executable, and resettable website environments for reproducible training and evaluation. Human-assisted mirrors demand per-site engineering, while generative construction can diverge from specified real websites, and capture-and-replay preserves observations with interaction coverage constrained by captured content. WebAnything is a fully automated framework that reconstructs source-grounded, reusable web environments from real URLs without per-site manual implementation or repair after initial setup and authorization. An Explorer-Builder collects browser-observable structures, resources, and interaction outcomes, and an independent verifier checks frozen candidates and guides local repair or targeted source recapture. The framework produces 244 website artifacts across 14 application domains and 53 categories, supporting 12,984 executed and accepted tasks. Compared with human-assisted WebHarbor, WebAnything achieved higher aggregate scores on three fidelity metrics, with 12 wins, two ties, and no losses in a blind audit. Fine-tuning Qwen3.5-9B on 5,856 interaction trajectories improved REAL success by 9.9 percentage points, Odysseys-Easy Rubric Average and Task Average by 6.25 and 6.32 percentage points, and WebArena-Verified Overall success by 13.33 percentage points under a fixed harness.

## Key points

- The framework reconstructs reusable web environments fully automatically from real URLs.
- It produced 244 website artifacts across 14 domains and 53 categories.
- It supports 12,984 executed and accepted tasks.
- A blind audit recorded 12 wins, 2 ties, and 0 losses versus WebHarbor on three fidelity metrics.
- Fine-tuning Qwen3.5-9B on 5,856 trajectories improved REAL success by 9.9 points.
- WebArena-Verified Overall success improved by 13.33 points.

## Details

Explorer-Builder gathers browser-observable structures, resources, and interaction outcomes. An independent verifier validates frozen candidates and guides local repair or targeted recapture. The resulting fidelity and downstream performance gains were measured under fixed harnesses rather than changed agent strategies.
