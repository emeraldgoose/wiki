# ForceHarness: Turning Force into Action and Physics for Frozen Frontier Models

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21099)
**Published**: 2026-11-16

## Overview

Frontier multimodal models can now control robots zero-shot from images, yet many manipulation tasks hinge on contact forces that images cannot show. Bringing force to an API-only frozen model raises two challenges: contact evolves much faster than an API model can decide, so the system must close the loop on force beneath the model; and a frozen model cannot interpret raw force streams, so force must be returned in a form the model can reason about. ForceHarness is a harness that lets frozen models use force without demonstrations or finetuning.

ForceHarness has three layers: self-terminating force primitives (force-calibrated, impedance-controlled skills with event-based termination that close the loop on force beneath the model), a physics translation layer that converts calibrated force into physical quantities the model can reason about, and a model-in-the-loop protocol that calls the model after each primitive so it can recover from failures. Across a real Franka FR3 arm without a wrist force/torque sensor, the public ForceBench suite in Isaac Lab, and the authors' new HiddenPhys benchmark in MuJoCo, frozen frontier models with ForceHarness match hand-written expert controllers and surpass policies trained on demonstrations. Project page: showlab.github.io/ForceHarness.

## Key points

- Real robot (Franka FR3, force estimated from joint torques, no wrist F/T sensor): GPT-6 Astra with ForceHarness goes from 0–1/10 to 8–10/10 successes on six contact tasks (whiteboard wiping, screw driving, eggplant peeling, USB insertion, weight sorting, soft/hard discrimination); GPT-6.1 Sol transfers at 6–10/10. Every task differs at p<0.01 (Fisher's exact test).
- ForceBench (8 contact-rich Isaac Lab tasks, evaluator kept, first 10 seeds): Astra and Sol reach 87.5% with ForceHarness and Claude Fable 5.2 reaches 75.0%, above 35.31% for the best trained policy. Paired force gains at McNemar's p=0.009/0.001, driven mainly by Drawer Pull going 0/10 → 10/10.
- HiddenPhys (new benchmark, 7 MuJoCo tasks with hidden physical variables — weight, center of mass, surface compliance — 20 test seeds each): Astra and Fable with ForceHarness both reach 135/140 with zero handle damage, matching the hand-written expert (136/140). Trained policies score 65–83/140 with frequent damage; adding wrist force to Diffusion Policy, π₀.₅, or ForceVLA brings no consistent gain.
- Within-harness effect of force: Astra 87/140 → 135/140, Fable 96/140 → 135/140, consistent across all eight OpenAI models tested; zero handle damage and 45% lower cost per episode for Astra. Under distribution shift on four tasks, ForceHarness models drop only 8%/5% (to 69/80 and 71/80) vs 16–19% without force and 17–25% for learned policies.
- Ablations: self-terminating primitives alone (event words, no force values) lift Sol success 0.63→0.77 and smaller models 0.18→0.54 and cut Lock handle damage 22/25 → 3/25; harness-computed L2 quantities lift smaller-model decision accuracy to 0.85 (p=0.023 over raw numbers) vs force plots 0.56; turn-by-turn feedback reaches 91% vs one-shot Code-as-Policies 23%→77% with the force interface.

## Details

- Architecture: (1) self-terminating force primitives — shared loop with impedance control, force-from-lead estimation, contact events, and per-backend constants; primitives cover making contact, holding force, finding/seating, turning under load, measuring, turning, moving by position; (2) physics translation layer — common recipe rendering held load, contact, pressing/sliding, insertion, pulling, and turning quantities at multiple rendering levels, with accuracy analysis; (3) model in the loop — frozen model called per primitive with text history and the last two turns of images.
- HiddenPhys tasks: Weight, CoM, Wipe, Stack, Peg, Latch, Lock; scoring includes lock handling with force-damage thresholds; evidence that tasks depend on force (position-only controller falls from 49 expert successes to 0 on Wipe and 50→25 on Weight over 50 seeds). Learned baselines: Diffusion Policy (per-task), π₀.₅ and ForceVLA (multi-task), 200 demos each.
- Limitations: model queries add seconds between primitives and preclude reactive coordination; a fixed primitive menu limits new motion laws; ablations run only in simulation. Authors: Ke Sun, Kevin Yuchen Ma, Yike Zhang, Yanzhe Chen, Mike Zheng Shou (Show Lab, National University of Singapore).
