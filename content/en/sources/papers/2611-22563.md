# CADMAGEDDON: High-Fidelity CAD Reconstruction with Local Inference

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22563)
**Published**: 2026-11-18

## Overview

CAD practitioners often work with proprietary parts they are unwilling to share with external services, and bringing reconstruction in-house must fit single-GPU systems and current-generation laptops. CADMAGEDDON is an open-source agent that reconstructs editable, feature-based CAD models from meshes — recovering the design intent (sketches, extrusions, revolutions, holes) that an engineer can readily edit — and it runs locally. The method decomposes this complex task into a chain of simple visual decisions about what to construct and what to correct; geometric tools support precise construction so the agent can focus on visual reasoning. It integrates with FreeCAD, bringing reconstructions into practitioners' existing workflows. Code: https://github.com/kulibinai/cadmageddon.

Using the mid-sized Qwen3.8-27B model, CADMAGEDDON outperforms prior approaches — including systems based on frontier models — in reconstruction quality on all the main benchmarks.

## Key points

- Five-benchmark results (Table 1): CADMAGEDDON reaches the highest IoU and GMS and lowest Chamfer distance on DeepCAD (>0.999 IoU), Fusion 360 (0.993), MCB (0.933), BenchCAD-1000 (0.999) and CADENA-Bench (0.981); MCB IoU rises from CADFather's 0.862 to 0.933 and CADENA-Bench from 0.918 to 0.981; GMS (surface-type similarity) rises from 0.705→0.818 (MCB) and 0.800→0.909 (CADENA-Bench).
- Executability: leaves at most 0.67% of parts without geometry; CADFit fails on 18.7–24.3% of DeepCAD/Fusion 360/MCB parts.
- BenchCAD leaderboard (Table 2, 64³ voxel IoU, 1000-part subset): CADMAGEDDON (Qwen3.8-27B, mesh) reaches 0.991 with 0.3% invalid predictions — vs frontier image+tools rows up to 0.963 (Claude Sonnet 5.5 max), image-only frontier at most 0.747, CADENA-RL 0.910 (17,900 parts) and CADFather 0.968. Scored from delivered meshes on the same 1000 parts: 0.995 vs CADFather 0.968 and CADENA 0.930.
- Margins over the strongest baseline (CADFather) are smallest on DeepCAD and grow on mechanical-part sets — consistent with CADMAGEDDON's advantage being surface structure (GMS gap).
- Local execution matters for proprietary parts: no cluster, no external API; integrates with FreeCAD.

## Details

- Method: mesh preprocessing → named geometric entities → geometric instruments (construction/correction tools) → construction-and-correction agent loop; the agent makes visual decisions, tools make precise measurements; implementation details in Sec. 3.6; call budget and tool-use analysis in Appendix A.
- Benchmarks: DeepCAD (300), Fusion 360 (300), MCB (300), BenchCAD (1000; plus BenchCAD-2 and FreeCAD integration, Sec. 4.4), CADENA-Bench (60). Metrics: IoU, GMS, median Chamfer distance (×10³), invalid-rate (IR). All methods except CADFit scored by the authors' evaluation code from prediction meshes.
