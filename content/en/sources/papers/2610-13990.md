## Overview

Sparse attention visits few keys, yet finding them can still require scoring every key with every indexer head. We introduce DoSA (Dots Sparse Attention), a lossless method for making this computation conditional while preserving exact token selection. Its central observation is that Top-KK selection requires sufficient evidence to reject a candidate, not necessarily its full score. A seed set supplies a lower bound on the winning threshold.
