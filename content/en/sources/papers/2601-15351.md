# OmniSpectra: A Unified Foundation Model for Universal Spectra Representation Learning

**Source**: [arXiv:2601.15351](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15351) · **Published**: 2026-10-23
**Authors**: Md Khairul Islam, Zeyu Xia, Adam S. Bolton, Judy Fox

## Overview

Standard sequence models encode position by token rank rather than physical coordinate, forcing practitioners to resample continuous physical signals such as spectra observed across heterogeneous instruments. OmniSpectra identifies this coordinate mismatch and proposes a wavelength-native foundation model: a continuous-coordinate masked autoencoder discarding integer pixel indexing. Mapping physical coordinates to a logarithmic basis turns multiplicative redshift scaling into an additive shift; in continuous rotary embeddings this shared shift cancels from relative token distances.

## Key points

- Treats coordinate mismatch as a structural limitation in cross-instrument representation learning.
- Uses continuous-coordinate masked autoencoding instead of token-rank positions.
- Logarithmic coordinate basis converts redshift scaling into uniform shift.
- Adds a resolution-gated Fourier decoder for physics-grounded anti-aliasing.
- Achieves SOTA across surveys/baselines with only 12.2M parameters.

## Details

Encoding physical structure reduces parameter dependence: physical coordinate-aware representations outperform larger models lacking such structure.
