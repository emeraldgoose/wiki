# Avatar Intelligence: Amplifying Human Agency Through Persistent Personal Agents

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03092](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03092) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Zheng Wu, Xinbei Ma, Yao Yao, Zhaoyuan Huang, Haowen Hu, Yijie Lu, Taolei Wang, Yifei Yang, Wuyue Xiong, Pengzhou Cheng, Zhuosheng Zhang

## Overview

Personal AI agents are increasingly capable of long-horizon tasks across applications, yet users often must invest substantial effort in specifying goals, resolving ambiguity, and verifying outcomes. The paper argues that progress should be measured by how effectively agents amplify human agency: enabling people to pursue goals with less routine supervision while retaining control over consequential decisions. Avatar Intelligence proposes persistent personal agents that represent users' goals, values, and constraints through sustained decision delegation across contexts.

## Key points

- Personal agents can handle long-horizon tasks.
- Users still bear effort in goals and verification.
- Agency amplification is the key success metric.
- Less routine supervision is needed.
- Consequential decisions remain under user control.
- Persistent agents model goals, values, and constraints.

## Details

The framework shifts evaluation from task success alone to empowerment of the user. A persistent personal agent learns durable preferences and boundaries rather than resetting each session. It delegates decisions over time while preserving user control where outcomes matter. The paper presents Avatar Intelligence as a paradigm for long-term personalization rather than another short-horizon assistant interface.
