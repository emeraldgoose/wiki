# VeriSpoof: Beyond Real or Fake with One Audio LLM

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.04634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04634) · **Published**: 2026-11-03
**Authors**: Yang Xiao, Ting Dang

## Overview

A speech deepfake detector estimates whether a recording is synthetic, but forensic analysts also ask which system produced it, whether recordings share a source, and what evidence supports each conclusion. The paper presents VeriSpoof, an audio language model that unifies five tasks with shared weights: deepfake detection, generator attribution (generator, acoustic model, and vocoder), recording-condition analysis, synthesized-span localization, and source verification. Each decision is supported by grounded rationales whose statements can be independently verified. Answers are scored by sentence likelihood, allowing detection and source verification to be thresholded without separate heads. Rationale statements can be checked against waveform measurements, span labels, or generator metadata, and counterfactual training pushes decisions to follow the stated evidence. On the 14 Speech-DF-Arena test sets, VeriSpoof-Plus achieves 2.14% pooled EER, third of 28 systems and first among open-weight systems. VeriSpoof-Base, trained only on official training splits of eight corpora, reaches 4.43% EER, less than half the next open-weight detector. With the same weights, VeriSpoof-Plus verifies whether recordings from generators unseen in training share a source at 6.2% EER. Controlled studies show the audio representation governs detection, the LLM governs recording comparison, and source supervision is necessary for neural-codec fakes. Statement-checkable rationales catch partial fakes missed by one-step answers (99.6% vs. 78.4% on PartialEdit) and determine decisions under unseen edits (99.5%). Training updates only 29.7M of 2.85B parameters, fitting a single 24 GB GPU with gradient checkpointing. Both models, inference code, a demo, and the VeriSpoof-Bench evaluation suite are released.

## Key points

- Goes beyond real/fake labels to include attribution, recording condition, span localization, and source verification.
- Uses shared weights and grounded, independently checkable rationales.
- Answer scoring by sentence likelihood avoids separate detection/source-verification heads.
- VeriSpoof-Plus reaches 2.14% pooled EER on Speech-DF-Arena (first among open-weight), and VeriSpoof-Base 4.43% on official splits.
- Partial-fake catch improves from 78.4% to 99.6% and unseen-edit decisions reach 99.5%.

## Details

Training updates only 29.7M of 2.85B parameters and fits a single 24 GB GPU with gradient checkpointing. Benchmarks cover seen and unseen generators and partial edits. Full result tables and model release information are in the linked source.
