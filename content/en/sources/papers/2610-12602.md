# Self-Preservation in Language Models

**Source**: Cho, Chunara & Behrouz, arXiv, 2026-10-08

**Lead**: Continual learning adapts pretrained models to new tasks, but the replay data supplied for preservation covers only part of what a model knows. This paper asks whether a model can preserve *itself*: using a frozen pre-update model to generate replay data from organized Task Banks, and even to choose which tasks to replay. Across Qwen3 models (0.6B–4B), generated replay protects held-out capabilities, combining banks strengthens protection, and incoming-task-informed target selection helps at 4B.

## Background

Fine-tuning a language model on a new task can degrade prior behavior (catastrophic forgetting). Standard continual-learning defenses—replay of stored examples, interference-aware updates—rely on supplied preservation evidence. But when original training data are unavailable, that evidence covers only a slice of the model's capabilities, and forgetting extends beyond the supplied tasks.

Recent work shows models can generate useful replay without their training data (LAMOL, Self-Synthesized Rehearsal, Self-Replay). This paper separates two design decisions: *how much* to rehearse versus *what* to rehearse, and tests whether broader target coverage and model-driven target selection protect behavior outside the replay inventory.

A key measurement insight: aggregate accuracy can hide forgetting. Newly corrected answers offset lost previously-correct answers, so the authors also report *Retention*—the fraction of initially correct answers that stay correct—on paired pre/post predictions.

## Task Banks and Task-Bank Self-Replay

A **Task Bank** is a compact inventory of preservation targets: each entry has a task name, a description of the behavior and input/output format, and optional demonstrations. Entries may overlap in skills.

**Task-Bank Self-Replay** pipeline:

1. **Select** entries to rehearse (random, bank-only model selection, or incoming-task-informed model selection) from the bank
2. **Generate** replay questions and answers with a frozen pre-update reference model
3. **Fine-tune** the learner with supervised loss on incoming-task examples mixed with generated replay

Selector, replay generator, and learner stay distinct roles even when sharing a checkpoint. Held-out tasks (examples, labels, descriptions, scores) are excluded from both bank construction and target selection, so held-out results measure protection *beyond* the inventory.

## Experiments

**Setup**: Qwen3-0.6B, 1.7B, 4B (plus Qwen3.5-4B in forgetting diagnostics). Incoming tasks: MedQA (1,024 examples, 1 epoch, lr 1e-5, 5 seeds) and C-STANCE from TRACE. Task Bank: MMLU, HellaSwag, GSM8K, BoolQ. Held-out: ARC-Challenge, WinoGrande, PIQA, SVAMP.

**Benchmarks**: Incoming-task accuracy gain plus prior-task accuracy change (percentage points) and Retention (correct-to-correct) on paired predictions.

**Results**:

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Averages conceal forgetting | Four-task mean net change +0.05pp hides 3.56% lost vs 3.61% gained answers |
| Broad replay beats single-target (C-STANCE, 4B) | Mean held-out Δ: SFT −4.34pp, single-task −3.99pp, real replay +0.86pp, self-replay +1.07pp; incoming gains all ≈ +27.8pp |
| Combining partial banks helps at all sizes | Combined Retention 88.70% / 93.28% / 96.01% (0.6B/1.7B/4B), +4.42/+3.32/+2.99pp over the better partial bank |
| Incoming-aware selection helps at 4B only | 95.69% vs 92.94% random (+2.75pp) and 93.42% bank-only, near full-bank level with 2 of 4 targets; no advantage at 0.6B/1.7B |
| Fixed example budget trades off | Replacing 59/256 incoming rows with replay: worst held-out −1.56 → −0.68pp, but MedQA gain +1.17 → +0.49pp |
| Lower learning rate helps both goals | Qwen3-1.7B on C-STANCE at 1e-7: +12.01pp incoming, 97.02% held-out Retention; at 1e-5 adaptation collapses |

Model-proposed bank expansion (beyond supplied targets) remains exploratory: a single-arm pilot shows negative accuracy changes.

## Relevance to Software Engineers

For teams fine-tuning LLMs on new domains (medical QA, stance detection, internal tools):

- **No stored history required**: a frozen copy of the current model generates its own regression set—useful when original training data are unavailable or cannot be retained
- **Measure retention, not just averages**: track per-task accuracy plus Retention of previously correct answers; means near zero can hide ~3.5% churn
- **Cover broadly, not deeply**: combining diverse replay targets beats more examples from one task; two well-chosen targets approach full-bank protection
- **Budget the trade-off**: under a fixed training budget, replay tokens displace new-task tokens—expect smaller new-task gains in exchange for preservation
- **Learning rate is a first-order lever**: 1e-7 beat 1e-5 on both acquisition and retention in these short-update regimes
