# OpenFlyV2: An onboard vision-language-action model for aerial perception and navigation

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39287)
**Date**: 2026-10-29


## Overview

Human cognition flexibly draws on a shared perceptual foundation to understand environments, attend to referenced entities, reason about spatial relationships, and act. Endowing aerial robots with an analogous functional organization remains challenging, as existing systems are often specialized for individual capabilities, dependent on offboard computation, or unable to sustain closed-loop operation on resource-constrained platforms. We present OpenFlyV2, a unified onboard vision-language-action architecture that uses a shared vision-language representation to support semantic understanding,  An onboard aerial brain closes the loop from human intent and perception to reasoning and physical action, enabling aerial robots to understand and autonomously fly in the real world.
