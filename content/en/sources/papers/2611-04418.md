# VidUEU-Agent: A Data-Curation Agent for Multimodal Understanding, Editing, and Unified Tasks

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.04418](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04418) · **Published**: 2026-11-03
**Authors**: Tengju Ru, Weitong Lian, Zecong Tang, Lingyi Meng, Haoran Li, Zhejun Cui, Yichen Zhu, Hangshuo Cao, Qi Kang, Yechi Liu, Kaixuan Wang, Yu-Jie Yuan, Chunwei Wang, Yu Zhang, Bo Dai

## Overview

High-quality multimodal instruction data is scarce and costly to annotate, even though raw videos provide abundant visual knowledge. VidUEU-Agent automates synthesis of instruction data from raw videos for understanding, editing, and unified multimodal tasks. Its four-stage pipeline filters low-quality shots through global perception, mines keyframes and multimodal signals, constructs metadata, and routes that metadata to synthesize instruction samples. More importantly, the agent framework can autonomously iterate the data distribution based on downstream training results, optimizing quality, balance, and training effectiveness. The resulting VidUEU-100K dataset contains 100K training samples across multiple domains and tasks.

## Key points

- The pipeline synthesizes instruction data from raw video material.
- It covers understanding, editing, and unified multimodal tasks.
- Global perception filters low-quality shots before downstream processing.
- Keyframes and multimodal signals are mined for metadata construction.
- Metadata is routed to synthesize instruction samples.
- The agent iterates the data distribution from downstream training results.

## Details

Scaling the pipeline yields VidUEU-100K, a large-scale dataset spanning multiple domains and tasks. Fine-tuning baseline models on the data yields significant and consistent gains across diverse benchmarks, validating the agent and dataset. The results are limited to the quality metrics and benchmarks used; raw-video distributions, privacy considerations, and task coverage should be audited before broader reuse.
