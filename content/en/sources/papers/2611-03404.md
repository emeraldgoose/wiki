# VidDub: Replacement-Aware Distillation for Two-Step Video Dubbing

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03404) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Yexin Liu, Yunlong Lin, Zehan Wang, Guangxin He, Xinli Xu, Jiajun Zha, Yaofu Liu, Yueze Wang, Bowen Ping, Yuanming Li, Amie Wang, Bernard Ghanem, Bing Li, Harry Yang

## Overview

Video dubbing synthesizes lip movements matching target speech while preserving speaker identity, head pose, and background of a source video. Existing editors are predominantly trained via self-reconstruction under original paired audio, even though the source mouth becomes a conflicting target when driving speech is replaced. The paper introduces replacement-aware cross-audio supervision: paired-audio samples use full-frame reconstruction, whereas unpaired replacement-audio samples mask out source-mouth reconstruction losses and supervise the mouth solely through audio-visual synchronization while preserving unmasked facial regions and background. Because offline dubbing provides the complete source video and target speech beforehand, the authors present VidDub, a full-context diffusion editor distilled into two denoising steps (2 NFE). Its replacement-aware teacher first learns accurate lip synchronization without regressing toward conflicting source mouth movements; matched trajectory regression transfers the teacher's CFG-guided trajectories to a two-step student; and distribution matching distillation recovers visual quality after step compression. The paper releases curated training data, VidDub-Bench, fixed evaluation manifests, preprocessing outputs, and unified evaluation code. Across three training seeds, masking out conflicting source-mouth reconstruction improves cross-audio Sync-C by 0.833, and the final two-step model combines strong lip synchronization with superior visual quality, identity preservation, and visual speech recognition across four benchmarks.

## Key points

- Self-reconstruction on paired audio conflicts with the source mouth when driving speech is replaced.
- Replacement-aware cross-audio supervision masks source-mouth loss for unpaired replacements and uses audio-visual sync for the mouth.
- VidDub is a full-context diffusion editor distilled to two denoising steps.
- A replacement-aware teacher learns synchronization, matched trajectory regression transfers CFG trajectories, DMD recovers visual quality.
- Masking conflicting source-mouth reconstruction improves cross-audio Sync-C by 0.833 across three seeds.

## Details

The benchmark releases data and evaluation tooling for paired and cross-audio settings. Performance is reported across identity preservation, visual quality, synchronization, and visual speech recognition on four benchmarks. Full results tables are in the linked source.
