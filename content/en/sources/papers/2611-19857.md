# PanDWM: Panoramic 4D Driving World Models

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19857)
**Published**: 2026-11-15

## Overview

Driving world models usually generate videos from six pinhole cameras with separate optical centres and small overlaps, so they must learn to keep those views consistent. PanDWM instead stitches the six views into one 360° panoramic band; camera boundaries then fall inside a single image, so cross-view consistency reduces to keeping the image continuous, and each pixel still records its camera ray.

Conditioned on layouts, a causal student generates the band window by window in four steps; it is distilled from a causal teacher and trained on its own rollouts. A feed-forward decoder lifts each frame along its rays into 3D Gaussians, and static Gaussians from neighbouring frames complete them into 4D scenes. On nuScenes, scored in one stitched 360° view, PanDWM achieves lower FID and FVD than multi-camera baselines at 8–46× their speed, keeps its 50-step teacher's FID, and lowers FVD by 22–25%. Code: https://github.com/AIGeeksGroup/PanDWM.

## Key points

- Single 360° panoramic band instead of six separate pinhole views: consistency = image continuity; per-pixel camera rays are preserved.
- Causal student generates band window by window in 4 steps, distilled from a 50-step causal teacher and trained on its own rollouts; teacher's FID is kept while FVD improves by 22–25%.
- Feed-forward decoder lifts frames into 3D Gaussians; static Gaussians from neighbouring frames complete them into 4D scenes.
- nuScenes (stitched 360° scoring): lower FID and FVD than multi-camera baselines at 8–46× their speed.
- Long-horizon self-generated rollouts and multi-view consistency evaluated across Val-150, Test-S, and Test-L splits; seams at camera boundaries are explicitly analyzed (appendix).

## Details

- Inputs: surround-view layouts and past frames; causal factorization over band windows; distillation from a 50-step teacher to a 4-step student with self-rollout training.
- 3D/4D lifting: per-pixel rays unproject the band into 3D Gaussians; neighbouring-frame static Gaussians complete dynamic content into 4D scenes.
- Evaluation: nuScenes Val-150 / Test-S / Test-L; FID/FVD in the stitched 360° view; inference cost at matched angular resolution; baselines reproduced (UniMLVG, OpenDWM (SD3.5), MagicDrive-V2, DiST-4D, OmniNWM, DreamForge, RAYNOVA).
- Caveats: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license; panoramic stitching assumes calibrated rigs; quality depends on the distillation and ray-accurate unprojection.
