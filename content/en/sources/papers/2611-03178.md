# CoworkOS:Scaling Agent Coworkers for Real-world Automation

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03178) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Jiajie Jin, Guanting Dong, Chengjun Pan, Xin Deng, Zhuohui Sheng, Guoqiang Wang, Tao Gui, Yutao Zhu, Yujia Chen, Shihong Yi, Binze Hu, Zhixiong Yang, Zhichao Sun, Rui Wang, Han Han, Mingyu Ma, Junhao Huang, Xiaotao Wang, Huicheng Jiang, Yuyang Hu, Xinyu Yang, Wanghan Xu, Yajie Yang, Zhecan Wang, Honglin Guo, Xiaoyu Xing, Xiaoqian Zhang, Yulun Zhu, Xiaoxi Li, Jiahang Lin, Shaofan Liu, Shihan Dou, Zhiheng Xi, Qi Zhang, Yu-Gang Jiang, See-Kiong Ng, Yang Zhang, Tat-Seng Chua, Ji-Rong Wen, Zhicheng Dou

## Overview

Rapid advances in cowork agents are shifting human-AI collaboration toward real-world automation that produces software, designs, and other artifacts. Productivity work demands both high-quality delivery on complex tasks and continued monitoring and decision-making over long horizons. CoworkOS is a general-purpose harness for long-horizon work that separates task strategy from execution management. The model decides actions, delegation, and acceptance, while the runtime maintains state and feedback. Its three-layer design targets strategy, execution, and verification for sustained coworker operation.

## Key points

- Cowork agents automate real-world productivity tasks.
- Tasks require long-horizon monitoring.
- High-quality delivery remains challenging.
- CoworkOS separates strategy from execution management.
- The model decides actions, delegation, and acceptance.
- The runtime maintains state and feedback.

## Details

The paper positions cowork agents as systems that must manage work over time rather than answer single requests. By separating task strategy from runtime execution management, CoworkOS lets the model reason about what to do while the harness preserves continuity and feedback. This architecture targets durable human-AI collaboration and artifacts produced across sessions. The framework generalizes beyond one type of cowork task.
