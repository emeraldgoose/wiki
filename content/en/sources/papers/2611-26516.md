# Plan4D: Generative Plannable 4D Worlds

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.26516](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26516) · **Published**: 2026-11-19 (v1)
**Authors**: Hangwei Zhang, Rujing Dang, Ruimin Lin, Jiahong Wu, Xiangxiang Chu, Chuanxia Zheng

## Overview

Most world models for future prediction optimize the aesthetic quality of generated video but offer only limited geometric consistency over time, because they lack explicit 4D modeling. Plan4D targets a "generative plannable 4D world model": given an observed scene context and text-specified actions, it predicts future RGB video together with dense 4D geometry — depth, camera poses, point clouds, and scene flow — in a single feed-forward pass.

Because training data pairing actions with dense 4D annotations is scarce, Plan4D exploits the complementary priors of a foundational video generator and a 3D reconstructor. Concretely, it augments a frozen Depth Anything 3 (DA3) with a token-only scene-flow head (an auxiliary DPT head) and learns a latent-to-geometry stitching interface that maps Wan video-VAE latents onto DA3 geometry tokens, then finetunes the stitched decoder and jointly trains video and 4D geometry. The result turns a pretrained video generator (LongCat-Video) into an actionable 4D world model whose RGB, depth, camera rays, point clouds, and scene flow are all read out from the same evolving latent state — no manual 4D annotations needed for action videos.

## Key points

- Task: generative plannable 4D worlds — joint future RGB video + dense 4D geometry (depth, cameras, point clouds, scene flow) conditioned on observed context and text actions.
- Architecture: frozen DA3 + auxiliary DPT scene-flow head; latent-to-geometry stitching adapter from video VAE latents to geometry tokens; stitched-decoder finetuning; joint video and 4D geometry training.
- Scene flow: both scene-flow variants remain competitive on all three benchmarks while using the smallest training set among compared methods; the RGB-based DA3 variant is strongest on the harder real dynamic benchmarks; the stitched variant is best on the controlled synthetic benchmark and near-top on Dynamic Replica.
- Stitched decoder preserves DA3 behavior: closer to RGB-based DA3/VGGT than prior aligned decoders (VIST3A-Stitched, Gen3R-VAE) across reconstruction, camera, and point-cloud metrics — e.g. ETH3D reconstruction Acc 0.2898 (DA3 0.2783, VGGT 0.2802), ScanNet++ Comp 0.1425 (DA3 0.1015), Dynamic Replica RRA@5 0.8987 (DA3 0.8971), Dynamic-RE10K camera AUC@30 0.5350 (DA3 0.5333); it slightly exceeds DA3 on Dynamic-RE10K camera.
- Qualitative results show cleaner motion boundaries, more stable articulated motion, and better global 3D structure than Any4D and MotionCrafter baselines.

## Details

The pipeline decouples acquisition of geometric priors from generation of future dynamics and reconnects them through the stitched latent world model. Appendix A shows frozen DA3 tokens already encode cross-frame motion cues. Training details cover per-dataset roles (synthetic and real-world), scene-flow head training, stitching training, decoder finetuning, and joint video+4D training, with ablations over the joint objective, adapter architecture (linear convolutional, residual bottleneck, axial-attention adapters), and finetuning strategy. Evaluation covers ETH3D, ScanNet++, Dynamic Replica, and Dynamic-RE10K across reconstruction accuracy/completeness/F-score and camera RRA/AUC metrics. Limitations and broader impact are discussed in appendices; project page: https://fogradio.github.io/plan4d-project.
