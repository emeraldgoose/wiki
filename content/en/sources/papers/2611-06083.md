# SleuthVLM: Do We Really Need Audio-in-Video for Omni Video Understanding?

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.06083](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06083) · **Published**: 2026-11-04
**Authors**: Peiran Wu, Fangfei Gou, Zhaoxi Mu, Changhao Zhang, Jian Liu, Weiqiang Wang, Yuntao Wang, Yuanchun Shi, Junxiao Shen

## Overview

Omni models answer video questions by jointly processing sampled frames and audio in one turn, with progress driven largely by scaling model size and omni training data. The paper asks whether a model needs to hear the audio directly at all. It presents SleuthVLM, an agent harness built on a VLM that never hears the audio directly: speech is transcribed into text, and the VLM sleuths the video on its own without a predefined workflow. On four omni benchmarks with videos up to 90 minutes, SleuthVLM achieves the best average accuracy, 75.0%, 2.6 points above Gemini 3.8 Flash, and tops the Video-MME-v2 leaderboard with 77.8%. With an open-source 27B VLM, it beats all open-source omni models. The authors draw three findings: SleuthVLM beats a model that only glances; hearing is not the bottleneck, agentic ability is; and omni understanding needs better agentic ability, not just more omni data for modality alignment. The work is presented as a simple but effective starting point for omni video understanding, concluding that better agents, not just bigger models that hear, are needed.

## Key points

- SleuthVLM never directly hears audio; speech is transcribed and a VLM investigates the video.
- No predefined workflow; the harness lets the VLM sleuth evidence autonomously.
- Best average accuracy 75.0% on four omni benchmarks (up to 90-minute videos), 2.6 points above Gemini 3.8 Flash.
- Tops Video-MME-v2 with 77.8%; an open 27B VLM variant beats all open-source omni models.
- Hearing is not the bottleneck; agentic ability is.

## Details

The approach emphasizes agent ability over modality alignment. Transcription plus VLM sleuthing replaces direct audio ingestion. Full benchmark, ablation, and harness details are in the linked source.
