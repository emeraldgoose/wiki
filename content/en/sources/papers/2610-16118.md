## Overview

Hour-long videos, streaming feeds, and movie-length content are pushing vision-language models (VLMs) past their breaking point—the standard recipe of encoding each frame into visual tokens yields sequences that grow linearly with video length, causing prohibitive memory consumption and inference latency. Existing approaches, such as token reduction and keyframe selection, attempt to shorten the token sequence but leave the O⁡(n)O(n) scaling intact, failing to resolve the fundamental bottleneck.
