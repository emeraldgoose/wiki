# GameRealm: Grounding Visual Learning in Interactive Games

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23140)
**Published**: 2026-11-17

## Overview

Visual foundation models are central to physical intelligence, yet their development remains constrained by fragmented training datasets, inconsistent annotation quality, and limited multimodal supervision. Modern video games offer visually rich environments, complex scene dynamics and interactive behaviors, and — crucially — game engines provide direct access to precise, game-native supervision (metric depth, camera poses, motion information) that is hard to acquire accurately at scale in the real world.

GameRealm is a large-scale, richly annotated dataset grounding visual observations in synchronized, game-native supervision: over 120 million RGB frames and 1,000 hours of gameplay across single-view, stereo, multi-view and panoramic recordings. It provides metric depth, camera parameters, synchronized audio, control inputs and 3D point trajectories. A unified data-collection pipeline combines human-operated interaction with agentic exploration for scalable acquisition. Across diverse visual learning tasks, GameRealm consistently strengthens visual foundation models in both visual generation and geometric learning. Project page: weiguangzhao.github.io/GameRealm. License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

## Key points

- Scale: 120M+ frames, 1,000+ hours, diverse environments and interaction scenarios, flexible views (single-view / stereo / multi-view / panoramic); annotations include metric depth, camera parameters, audio, keyboard/mouse inputs, and point tracking (with a proposed 2D/3D point-tracking benchmark in eight subcategories under Interaction and Locomotion).
- Stereo generalization (Fast-FoundationStereo + GameRealm, 20k steps, 16×A800, zero-shot on real-world benchmarks): error reduced on 12 of 13 reported metrics; largest relative reduction 14.3% on ETH3D BP-3 (0.49% → 0.42%), KITTI 2015 D1 also improves.
- Positioning: unlike SeKai-Game (no depth) and OmniWorld-Game (π³-estimated, not ground-truth, camera trajectories; no synchronized multiview/audio), GameRealm provides game-engine ground truth via dedicated plugins, plus human-operated and agentic exploration collection.
- Utility demonstrated across the stack: fine-tuning world models on recorded player inputs (predicting view evolution under keyboard/mouse commands), stereo and multi-view reconstruction, and point tracking.

## Details

- Data collection: game-engine plugins access ground truth (depth from the renderer, camera ground truth, action inputs); human operators capture context-dependent interactions, agentic exploration systematically navigates free-roaming environments; clips with visual degradation or inconsistency are filtered; modalities kept synchronized.
- Tasks: single-view/multi-view/panoramic interactive world models (fine-tuned with recorded player inputs to predict view evolution), stereo reconstruction (Fast-FoundationStereo fine-tuning with source-aware weighted sampling), multi-view reconstruction, point tracking (new 2D/3D benchmark); evaluated on Middlebury, ETH3D, KITTI 2015 and other real-world benchmarks.
