# HuGe: Scaling Humanoid Generalist Vision-Language-Action Models with Egocentric Experience

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.34186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34186) · **Published**: 2026-10-25
**Authors**: Yang Liu, Jiacheng Liu, Hengtao Li, et al.

## Overview

Extending Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) to humanoid robots requires coordinated whole-body control and diverse demonstrations that are costly to collect in real-world scenarios. Although egocentric human demonstrations provide a complementary source of task experience, differences in visual embodiment and missing robot proprioception complicate transfer to humanoid policies.

HuGe is a family of Humanoid Generalist VLAs pre-trained on over 2,000 hours of egocentric human and robot data. It targets whole-body control by predicting latent motion tokens for a pretrained whole-body controller together with hand-control signals.

## Key points

- Introduces HuGe, a family of humanoid generalist VLAs pre-trained on 2,000+ hours of egocentric data.
- Uses three representative VLA architectures to predict latent motion tokens plus hand-control signals.
- Employs a three-stage pipeline: egocentric pre-training, humanoid adaptation, and downstream fine-tuning.
- Builds pseudo-proprioceptive states via simulation playback during pre-training and re-renders arm appearance during fine-tuning to aid embodiment transfer.
- Achieves state-of-the-art on HumanoidArena and improves pick-and-drop success on Unitree G1 with visually adapted egocentric demonstrations.

## Details

The approach couples data scale with embodiment bridging: pseudo-proprioception substitutes missing robot state during pre-training, while re-rendering aligns human and robot arm appearance. The result is stronger humanoid VLA performance with less costly real-world data collection.
