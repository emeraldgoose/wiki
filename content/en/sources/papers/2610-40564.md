# Training Foundation Models with a Single Algorithm

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40564)
**Date**: 2026-10-29


## Overview

Foundation models are typically trained in stages: pretraining, midtraining, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and reinforcement learning (RL), with changes in both data and learning algorithms. We show that this algorithmic separation is not fundamental. By deriving suitable RL objectives as maximum-likelihood fitting to model-generated target distributions, we place all of these stages under a single likelihood-based training algorithm, unified training. This perspective turns the foundation-model training lifecycle into a problem of choosing which data to train on, in what proportions, and when Foundation models are conventionally trained through a sequence of stages: pretraining, midtraining, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and reinforcement learning (RL) (deepseek2025v3; olmo2025olmo3).
