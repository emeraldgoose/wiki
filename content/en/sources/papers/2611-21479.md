# LynnReal-Hand: Unlocking World Interaction with Hand Control

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21479)
**Published**: 2026-11-17 (v2)

## Overview

LynnReal-Hand is a hand-controlled video world model that unifies precise interaction modeling with efficient streaming generation. Interactive video world models enable exploration via text and camera controls, but direct object interaction demands finer control — hands are the natural interface, though translating continuous hand movements into coherent visual responses is hard due to complex hand-object dynamics and real-time compute demands.

## Key points

- **100K interaction clips** spanning diverse real and synthetic environments with hand-aware supervision for fine-grained hand motions and object interactions.
- **Causal flow map** for few-step generation, plus **region-aware score distillation** combining complementary video and image priors to refine interaction dynamics and visual appearance.
- **Ultra-Fast-VAE**: a compact video decoder preserving high reconstruction fidelity at substantially lower compute.
- **HandBench results**: full-sequence FVD 181.67 and MANO Dice 93.02; three-step causal model MANO Dice 92.49.
- **Real-time**: 540p streaming on a single H200 at **18.03 FPS**, 741ms latency, 34.89GB peak GPU memory.

## Details

The model's design principle is that direct object interaction needs a richer action interface than text or camera alone. The region-aware score distillation combines video priors (temporal interaction dynamics) with image priors (spatial appearance) to fix the compounding errors of few-step generation. Ultra-Fast-VAE reduces decode cost without hurting fidelity. Code, weights, and 100K-clip dataset will be released.
