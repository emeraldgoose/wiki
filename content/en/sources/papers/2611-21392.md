# VideoNexus: Video Perception Beyond the Reasoning Context

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21392)
**Published**: 2026-11-16

## Overview

Hour-scale video understanding strains the reasoning context of frontier multimodal models: stuffing frames into the planner piles up pixels that are re-read every turn, inflating cost without commensurate accuracy. VideoNexus decouples perception from reasoning — the planner stays in text, while a separate perception pathway watches the video and returns compact evidence (text plus a few evidence-card images). This "perceive elsewhere, reason in text" split lets the system use many more frames than fit a planner context without paying their token cost every turn.

On hour-scale LVBench (1,549 questions over 103 videos), VideoNexus with Gemini 3.8 Flash reaches 88.6% accuracy at $0.140 per question — no shared-backbone method is both cheaper and more accurate. On Video-GAIA, with Kimi K3 as planner and perception model, it answers 163/271 questions vs 140 for the official ReAct agent (+8.5 points, concentrated on L2).

## Key points

- LVBench main comparison (Gemini 3.8 Flash, 1,549 questions): VideoNexus 88.6% (1,372/1,549) at $0.140/q, +3.6 points over Static 1 fps ingestion (85.0%, $0.172; 90 won / 34 lost, McNemar p=5e-7), +7.2 over the VideoSeek re-run (81.4%, $0.188; 153 won / 42 lost, p=5e-16), and +1.1 over Gemini Agentic (87.5%, $0.319) at 44% of the cost (not significant, p=0.10).
- Efficiency: 621 video frames processed natively with audio vs 123 still frames for VideoSeek; planner sees only 3.3 evidence-card images per question; 9.7 model calls per question (6.1 planner turns + 3.6 perception calls) vs 18.6 for VideoSeek; output tokens 12.4k vs 26.2k per question (output billed at 5× fresh-input price).
- Cost breakdown: input tokens are similar (118.2k VideoSeek vs 118.7k VideoNexus); the cost gap comes from output volume. Repricing VideoSeek at the cheapest image tier gives $0.140 — a pricing sensitivity analysis, not a measured configuration.
- Video-GAIA (Kimi K3, 271 questions, LLM-judged): VideoNexus 60.2% overall vs 51.7% ReAct, gains concentrated on L2 (+11.5), requiring entity location in video plus web identification; on L1 the two are within one question. (Both arms developed web/crop tools used on these questions — read as a transfer result, not a calibrated margin.)
- Leads in five of six LVBench categories (+2.7 reasoning, +3.6 summarization; temporal grounding is the exception); 122 of VideoNexus's 177 errors are also errors of both Static and Gemini Agentic, so much of the headroom is shared.

## Details

- Method: coupled vs decoupled multimodal contexts (Sec. 3.1); VideoNexus perceives video natively (with audio) and returns text evidence plus evidence cards; a "Finding protocol" for targeted retrieval; native timeline addressing and implementation. Ablations on a dev-200 stratified subset; main comparison uses the full set.
- Setup: LVBench categories ER (560), EU (526), KIR (196), Rea (147), Sum (55), TG (65); Video-GAIA L1 62 / L2 209; cost reported at list prices with cold-start repricing of cross-question caches; ASR cost excluded.
