# Latent Reasoning in Continuous Space for Unified Multimodal Models

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.42149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42149) · **Published**: 2026-10-27
**Authors**: Byungwoo Jeon, Yoonwoo Jeong, Hyunseok Lee, Minsu Cho, Jinwoo Shin

## Overview

Unified Multimodal Models succeed on diverse understanding and generation tasks but struggle to use chain-of-thought reasoning effectively for image generation. As a result, reasoning-augmented UMMs often fail to inherit test-time scaling: increasing the reasoning budget does not yield smooth gains comparable to LLMs.

LARC proposes that the limitation comes from a modality mismatch between discrete token-space reasoning and continuous image generation. It performs reasoning in continuous latent space, aligning text and image reasoning rather than requiring explicit tokens.

## Key points

- Identifies a discrete-continuous modality mismatch in reasoning-augmented UMMs.
- Proposes latent reasoning in continuous space.
- Extends continuous latent reasoning beyond LLMs to multimodal generation.
- Trains on 1M interleaved samples from multiple multimodal datasets.
- Evaluates on GenEval, DPG-Bench, and OneIG-Bench.
- Reports gains over base and reasoning-augmented UMMs.
- Argues reasoning should operate in continuous space for generation tasks.

## Details

Generation-only models can struggle with complex semantics requiring language-based reasoning, while token-based reasoning does not transfer well to image generation. LARC mitigates this gap by aligning reasoning and generation in latent space.

Building on Coconut-style latent reasoning, it is instantiated on BAGEL with interleaved samples from several sources. The framework treats continuous-space reasoning as the key to smoother test-time scaling for multimodal generation.
