# UniRestorer++: Scalable Universal Image Restoration with Multi-Granularity Degradation Modeling

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.06042](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06042) · **Published**: 2026-11-04
**Authors**: Jingbo Lin, Zhilu Zhang, Fan Li, Renjing Pei, Wenbo Li, Hang Xu, Hongzhi Zhang, Wangmeng Zuo

## Overview

Universal image restoration aims to handle diverse degradations within a single model, but a reliable scaling strategy remains underexplored. Increasing backbone depth and width can lead to performance saturation, while degradation-aware specialization depends on accurate degradation estimation and is vulnerable to errors on out-of-distribution and real-world inputs. The paper proposes UniRestorer++, a scalable framework based on multi-granularity degradation modeling. It progressively refines degradation groups and introduces group-specific experts, coupling capacity growth with specialization. Degradation-estimation-based routing selects a root-to-leaf path, while granularity-estimation-based routing chooses a level along that path to balance specialization and robustness. Spatially dense degradation representation maps further enable region-aware routing for spatially non-uniform corruptions. The authors also develop CDD-50, a benchmark covering 50 degradation categories from single to penta-degradation. Experiments show approximately log-linear PSNR trends over the evaluated model-size range and stronger scaling gains on out-of-distribution and real-world data than vanilla MoE scaling. UniRestorer++ achieves state-of-the-art performance across benchmarks and demonstrates zero-shot generalization and few-shot transfer on real-world images.

## Key points

- Scaling backbone size alone can saturate universal restoration performance.
- Degradation-aware specialization is sensitive to estimation errors, especially OOD/real inputs.
- Multi-granularity routing progressively refines groups and adds group-specific experts.
- Two routing criteria balance path selection (degradation estimate) with granularity (robustness).
- Spatially dense degradation maps enable region-aware routing for non-uniform corruptions; CDD-50 has 50 degradation categories.

## Details

Evaluation includes PSNR scaling trends over model size, zero-shot generalization, and few-shot real-world transfer. Benchmark details and architecture specifics are in the linked source.
