# On-Policy Self-Distillation with Teacher Evolution

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.38637](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38637) · **Published**: 2026-10-28
**Authors**: Shufan Shen, Zhongni Hou, Chao Bi, Yufei Zhang, Guojun Yin, Shuhui Wang

## Overview

On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) aligns a student model's token-level distribution with that of its self-teacher, where the teacher is prompted with privileged context. This offers a way to mitigate reward sparsity in sequence-level post-training. In practice, however, OPSD often fails to achieve continuous performance gains during training. The paper identifies a limited self-teaching capability as the underlying cause: the model generates unreliable guidance on certain samples.

To address this, the authors propose Teacher-Evolution Self-Distillation (TESD). The method is designed to evolve the teacher's guidance so it remains useful as training progresses. The central claim is that making self-distillation stable requires improving the reliability of the guidance produced by the teacher, rather than only increasing the training signal.

## Key points

- OPSD mitigates reward sparsity by using privileged context to guide on-policy distillation.
- Performance gains frequently stall because the self-teacher provides unreliable guidance on some samples.
- The failure is attributed to limited self-teaching capability rather than only optimization issues.
- TESD evolves the teacher to produce more reliable guidance during post-training.
- Stable self-distillation therefore depends on teacher quality, not only on the distillation objective.

## Details

The paper situates OPSD among post-training approaches for reasoning-capable language models and argues that sequence-level rewards are often too sparse to support steady improvement. Self-distillation provides a denser token-level signal, but its effectiveness assumes the teacher's guidance is trustworthy. Since the teacher is the same model in a different context, it can confidently generate misleading guidance. TESD changes this dynamic by evolving the teacher over training, aiming to keep the guidance reliable as the student improves.
