# MovieRoll: A Systematic Framework for Rolling Out Long-Form Videos

## Overview

Streaming video generation must sustain quality beyond the training horizon while handling self-generated history and growing cache costs. MovieRoll, from Peking University, Tencent Hunyuan, and HKUST, is a base-to-distilled framework that connects streaming fine-tuning, long-rollout distillation, and long-horizon inference into one pipeline for minute-long, event-by-event video generation.

The framework first establishes a robust multi-step base model through Window-wise Teacher Forcing (WinTF), which jointly denoises chunks at progressive noise levels with bidirectional attention within each window and causal access to preceding history. To retain historical context efficiently, hierarchical KV-cache compression (HieraKV) exploits layer-wise attention patterns, retaining full history in shallow layers and sink-plus-recent history in deeper layers — reducing cache memory by up to 78% while improving generation quality.

Building on that base, cumulative long tuning with distribution matching distillation (CuLT-DMD) holds parameters fixed throughout each long self-rollout and averages local gradients before updating, so each update combines supervision across temporal positions while keeping only one local computation graph. To extend generation beyond the training horizon, KV recomputation bounds context for both models, while guidance annealing (AnnealCFG) reduces guidance toward the clean endpoint to stabilize MovieRoll-Base. FBCA attention and PS-VAE decoding support efficient training and streaming output.

On HeliosBench, MovieRoll-Base remains competitive at 30s after training on videos of up to 15s, and MovieRoll-Distilled achieves the highest total scores among compared methods across single- and multi-event evaluations up to 60s, combining sustained naturalness with active motion.

## Key points

- Window-wise Teacher Forcing (WinTF): joint denoising of chunks at progressive noise levels, bidirectional attention within a window, causal access to prior history.
- HieraKV: layer-wise KV-cache compression — full history in shallow layers, sink + recent history in deeper layers; up to 78% cache memory reduction with improved quality.
- CuLT-DMD: cumulative long tuning with distribution matching distillation; fixed parameters during long self-rollouts, averaged local gradients, one local computation graph per update — enabling few-step generator training on long rollouts.
- Long-horizon inference: KV recomputation bounds context for both models; AnnealCFG anneals classifier-free guidance toward the clean endpoint for stability.
- Systems support: Flash Block-wise Causal Attention (FBCA) for training throughput and PS-VAE for parallel streaming decode.
- Result: MovieRoll-Base stays competitive at 30s after training on ≤15s clips; MovieRoll-Distilled posts the highest total HeliosBench scores up to 60s (single- and multi-event).
- Project page, code, and weights are public (Tencent-Hunyuan/MovieRoll; huggingface.co/yunyangge/MovieRoll).

## Details

**Method.** MovieRoll jointly denoises a rolling window of latent chunks at progressively increasing noise levels. Fully denoised chunks are emitted, and their KV states are cached for subsequent generation; attention is bidirectional within the window and causal to preceding history. WinTF trains MovieRoll-Base with parallel window supervision conditioned on clean history and event prompts. HieraKV allocates cache budget per layer according to measured attention patterns. After ODE initialization, CuLT-DMD distills the base into a few-step generator: parameters are frozen during each long self-rollout, local gradients are accumulated and averaged across temporal positions, and a single update is applied — avoiding the memory cost of backpropagating through an entire rollout. At inference, KV caches are rebuilt at a history threshold or at prompt switches (keeping both models' context bounded), and AnnealCFG gradually reduces guidance during base-model denoising to counter long-horizon drift. FBCA (block-wise causal attention kernel) and PS-VAE (parallel streaming VAE decoder) round out training and streaming-output efficiency. A streaming video prompt rewriting skill and threshold-triggered KV recomputation handle extrapolation and interactive prompt changes.

**Results.** MovieRoll-Base, trained on videos up to 15s, remains competitive at 30s on HeliosBench. MovieRoll-Distilled achieves the highest total scores among compared methods in both single-event and multi-event evaluations up to 60s, sustaining subject identity and natural motion across successive event prompts (Figure 1 demonstrates continuous identity across six events, one prompt every 10s). Cache memory drops by up to 78% with HieraKV while quality improves.
