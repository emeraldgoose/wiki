# Why Gated DeltaNet Survives 4-Bit Quantization: NVFP4 W4A4 for the Recurrent Half of a Hybrid 27B LLM

Source: Sergii Kozyrev and Davyd Maiboroda, arXiv:2609.04098, Sep 3, 2026. 14 pages, 2 figures, 6 tables.

**Lead**: Hybrid LLMs pair softmax attention with linear-recurrent layers like Gated DeltaNet (GDN), whose fixed-size state summarizes context. Community 4-bit quantizations of Qwen3.8-27B (48 GDN + 16 attention layers) left the GDN block — especially decay and write-strength gates — in 8/16-bit precision, on the intuition that recurrence accumulates errors over long contexts. This paper tests that intuition by building **Minima**: NVFP4 W4A4 on **all 496 linear layers**, GDN included. Across perplexity at 4K/32K, MMLU-Pro, GSM8K, AIME'25, GPQA-Diamond, LiveCodeBench, and RULER retrieval to 64K, Minima matches BF16 within seed noise (**−0.52** 5-task average) while being the smallest (**17.5 GiB**) and fastest-prefill (**+14–19%**) recipe compared — and its 32K perplexity gap shrinks with position.

## Four-part mechanism: why the recurrent half is the easy half

| Mechanism | Finding |
|---|---|
| Block scaling | NVFP4's 16-element scaling localizes residual-stream outliers, equalizing activation error across layer roles |
| Gate robustness | The "fragile" gate projections are least sensitive — softplus/exp and sigmoid compress ~11% GEMM error to **~2%** output error |
| Recurrence stability | Delta-rule recurrence holds injected noise at a flat plateau over 32K tokens and forgets a state impulse within hundreds of steps (each write overwrites along the current key direction) |
| Cost amortization | Per-token quantization cost washes out with context instead of compounding |

Two engineering fixes ship with the recipe: repairing the global-scale mismatch when per-module-calibrated NVFP4 checkpoints are served by kernels fusing modules into one GEMM, and shipping calibrated FP8 KV-cache scales (performance-free).

## Caveats

All numbers are from the arXiv abstract; full-text tables (per-task breakdowns, seed-noise bands) were not re-verified. Results are for one hybrid architecture (Qwen3.8-27B) and the NVFP4 format — transfer to other recurrent variants or MXFP4-style formats is not established here.

## Relevance to SW engineers

- Question inherited caution: "recurrence must accumulate error" was plausible, untested, and wrong here — the delta rule actively forgets perturbations.
- The practical recipe is simple: quantize everything, ship KV scales. The value is knowing it is safe, with a mechanism, not just a benchmark.
- Watch for calibration/serving mismatches: per-module scales vs. fused-kernel GEMMs silently disagree — this class of bug bites any quantized deployment.
- Per-token error that washes out with context (rather than compounding) is the property to test before quantizing any stateful layer.
