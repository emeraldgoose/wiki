# Youtu-Parsing-Omni: One Encoder, One Schema, All Modalities

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.39301](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39301) · **Published**: 2026-10-29
**Authors**: Yunhang Shen

## Overview

Youtu-Parsing-Omni is an omni-modal parser that converts documents, images, audio, and video into structured records grounded in space and time, providing a perception layer for agents that read, locate, and cite evidence across modalities. Its design rests on one encoder for every modality and one schema for every task. Youtu-Omni-Encoder is initialized from a pretrained language model to strengthen text reading, encodes all modalities with shared weights in a unified space-time coordinate system, and includes a few fusion layers so video frames can attend to concurrent audio, bringing cross-modal fusion and temporal alignment into perception itself. OmniSchema unifies outputs of all ten parsing tasks, allowing a single self-evolving post-training procedure, OmniSchema-Aware On-Policy Distillation, to provide a shared token-role-based objective across tasks. In each round the model is trained on its own rollouts against token-level targets from a reference-conditioned copy of itself, then refreshed, so parser and supervision co-evolve. With a single 5 B-parameter checkpoint, Youtu-Parsing-Omni achieved state-of-the-art performance on OmniDocBench v1.6 (96.96 Overall) and led all open-weight models on OmniParsingBench (75.08 Avg.), with its advantage concentrated on audio and video. It also achieved the best overall chart-parsing score (95.02) and highest exact chemical-fingerprint agreement among compared systems, and matched a dedicated optical-music model in character-level transcription of typeset sheet music while staying far ahead of general-purpose systems.

## Key points

- One encoder handles documents, images, audio, and video.
- A unified space-time coordinate system grounds records in space and time.
- Cross-modal fusion lets video frames attend to concurrent audio.
- OmniSchema unifies outputs across ten parsing tasks.
- Self-evolving on-policy distillation co-evolves parser and supervision.
- A single 5B checkpoint achieved 96.96 Overall on OmniDocBench v1.6.
- It led open-weight models on OmniParsingBench with 75.08 average.

## Details

Shared weights and one schema reduce task-specific fragmentation while post-training uses token-role targets from a reference-conditioned copy. The gains are especially strong for audio and video parsing relative to other open-weight models.
