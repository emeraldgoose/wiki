# ScaleVLA: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models to 552 Billion Parameters

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21607)
**Published**: 2026-11-16

## Overview

ScaleVLA connects a 552B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash backbone to a trainable action DiT and systematically compares VLMs from 2B to 552B under a shared training setup. The question: can frontier-scale representations serve as a stronger foundation for robot learning? The answer, per the paper's results, is yes — frontier VLMs are not only reasoning agents but powerful representational foundations for efficient and generalizable robot policies.

## Key points

- **Sample efficiency**: just 10 demonstrations outperform a 2B backbone trained with 50 (87.8% vs 85.6%).
- **Generalization**: LIBERO-PRO success reaches **45.1%**, compared with at most 31.3% for smaller backbones.
- **Precise manipulation**: real-world charger insertion improves from 4/10 → **9/10**, gains concentrated in contact-sensitive alignment.
- **Latency**: despite scale, **216 ms median latency** per action chunk.
- **Backbone scale**: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (552B) — first open-frontier-scale VLA comparison of this size.

## Details

The paper systematically compares VLMs from 2B to 552B under a shared setup and shows gains concentrated in sample efficiency, OOD generalization, and contact-sensitive precision — three capabilities that matter most for deployed robot foundation models. The trainable action DiT keeps the 552B backbone's general knowledge while adapting it to physical action. Project leader: Zekun Qi. Tech Report.
