# Open-HumanWAM: An Open Recipe for Scaling Human and Robot Experience

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.06817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06817) · **Published**: 2026-11-05
**Authors**: Juncheng Ma, Jiaxin Li, Jiankai Tu, Bo Liang, Guodong Lv, Yufan Deng, Junzhe Tang, Liangzhu Yang, Chen Bian, Shukai Gong, Yintianrun Zhang, Zicong Cao, Baohao Zhu, Jianxin Bi, Tengfei Yan, Yinyuan Zhang, Kewei Zhang, Xinglong Chen, Kaiqi Chen, Duomin Wang, Xiaotian Tang, Liang Tan, Li Li, Yuan Xu, Haosen Yang, Yuqi Wang, Jingfei Xia, Songyan Huang, Zhiyang Dou, Zhen Dong, Enze Xie, Tat-Seng Chua, Daquan Zhou

## Overview

Egocentric human video is a scalable source of manipulation priors for robot learning, but turning raw data scale into generalizable robot policies requires rigorous data curation and training recipes. Open-HumanWAM provides the first fully open recipe for scaling egocentric pretraining to 50,000 hours.

It is anchored by Open-HumanWAM-50K, a curated egocentric video corpus densely annotated with hand motion and natural language. Systematic closed-loop simulation evaluations uncover a log-linear scaling law linking pretraining hours to downstream robot success rates. HumanWAM-Vanilla, pretrained exclusively on 50,000 hours of egocentric human video, reaches strong RoboTwin 2.0 and RoboDojo results after robot post-training.

To expand capability, Open-HumanWAM also explores joint human-robot pretraining through HumanWAM-Goal, a visual-goal-conditioned world-action model. Hybrid data scaling unlocks broad zero-shot generalization and sets higher state-of-the-art levels on simulation, while exhibiting emergent embodied in-context learning on physical robots. The corpus, code, checkpoints, and evaluation suites are open-sourced for reproducible scaling research.

## Key points

- Provides an open recipe and 50,000-hour egocentric pretraining corpus.
- Annotates hand motion and natural language densely.
- Reports a log-linear scaling law between pretraining hours and robot success rates.
- HumanWAM-Vanilla reaches strong simulation performance after post-training.
- HumanWAM-Goal adds visual-goal-conditioned joint human-robot pretraining.
- Hybrid scaling improves zero-shot generalization and physical-robot adaptation.
