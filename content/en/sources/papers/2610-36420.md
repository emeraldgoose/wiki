# SO-101 Bench: Accessible Spatial Manipulation

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36420)
**Date**: 2026-10-27


## Overview

Low-cost arms such as the SO-101 have become the most common entry point to robot learning, yet no released policy performs spatially constrained, language-conditioned tasks on them out of the box, and existing SO-101 benchmarks run only on physical hardware with simple tasks and few held-out objects. We introduce SO-101 Bench, a simulated benchmark for language-conditioned spatial manipulation built on an Isaac Lab digital twin of a real SO-101 setup.
