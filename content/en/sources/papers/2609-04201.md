# Scal3R: Learning Efficient Multi-Relative Pose Query for Scalable Online 3D Reconstruction

Source: Chin-Yang Lin and 7 other authors, arXiv:2609.04201, Sep 3, 2026. ECCV 2026. Project page at linjohnss.github.io/scal3r/.

**Lead**: Online 3D reconstruction models degrade on long videos: regressing every pose relative to a **fixed first-frame anchor** forces extrapolation far beyond the training distribution, and small drifts compound into geometric collapse. The paper's key observation is a decoupling — **per-frame depth stays stable while only the global pose head breaks**; local geometry is intact. **Scal3R** therefore reframes online reconstruction as **multi-reference relative pose querying**: lightweight learnable tokens (~1% of parameters) injected via asymmetric attention into a **completely frozen backbone** query poses relative to multiple past keyframes, with online pose-graph optimization and loop closure suppressing long-range drift. It converges in **8 hours on a single GPU**, cuts average **ATE by over 60% on KITTI** versus the online baseline, and is state-of-the-art across Virtual KITTI, Sintel, TUM-Dynamic, ScanNet, and 7-Scenes.

## Why freezing works

The diagnosis dictates the fix. Because the backbone's local geometry survives the failure, retraining it would waste compute and risk breaking what works — so only the pose-querying tokens train. Querying against **multiple past keyframes** instead of one distant anchor keeps every estimate near the training distribution, and the pose-graph + loop-closure layer handles the residual drift that any incremental method accumulates.

## Relevance to SW engineers

- When a pipeline fails at long horizons, check **which stage actually degrades** before retraining everything — here depth was fine and only poses drifted, so 1% of parameters fixed it.
- Replace single-anchor relative estimation with multi-reference queries wherever drift compounds (odometry, tracking, mapping).
- Frozen backbone + tiny trainable adapter is the cheapest path to SOTA adaptation: 8 GPU-hours is within reach of small teams.
- Loop closure remains the backstop for any incremental estimator — budget for it in online designs.
