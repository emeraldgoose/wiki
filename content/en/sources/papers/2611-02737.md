# MoviEdit: Long-form Multi-Shot Video Editing Made Easy

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.02737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02737) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Suhyeon Lee, Zhengang Li, Soo Ye Kim, Peihao Zhu, Jong Chul Ye, Chun-Hao Paul Huang, Duygu Ceylan, Yan Kang, Hyeonho Jeong

## Overview

Long-form multi-shot video editing requires an intended edit to remain coherent across abrupt changes in scene, viewpoint, and subject appearance, while processing substantially longer visual context than conventional single-shot editors. MoviEdit is an end-to-end framework for scalable long-form multi-shot video editing that jointly addresses data, modeling, and evaluation. To provide cross-shot supervision at scale, it automatically generates 87,000 paired editing videos spanning synchronized multi-view footage, real-world multi-shot videos, and generated multi-shot content, without manual annotation. Architecturally, MoviEdit uses a cascaded bidirectional-autoregressive design.

## Key points

- The framework targets coherence across multiple video shots rather than single shots.
- Automatic data generation produces 87,000 paired editing videos without manual annotation.
- Data spans synchronized multi-view, real-world multi-shot, and generated content.
- A bidirectional stage first creates a globally coherent editing draft in compressed space.
- The initial draft is autoregressively propagated and refined over the full video.
- A dedicated benchmark, MoviEdit-Bench, supports systematic evaluation.

## Details

The framework directly addresses three challenges: providing enough cross-shot supervision, modeling long-form context, and benchmarking. Extensive experiments show that MoviEdit substantially outperforms existing open-source models and performs on par with industry frontier models across text-guided and image-guided editing. MoviEdit-Bench is introduced for systematic evaluation, and the project page provides complete results and benchmark access. The reported results should be interpreted within the evaluated edit types and footage categories, since long-form video editing settings can vary widely.
