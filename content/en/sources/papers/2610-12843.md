# APEX: Asynchronous Prefix Denoising for Extreme Compression

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.12843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12843) · **Published**: 2026-10-09
**Authors**: David Dinkevich, Nisan Chiprut, Yoav HaCohen, Dani Lischinski

## Overview

Fewer latent tokens promise cheaper diffusion. Yet aggressive latent compression creates a reconstruction-generation dilemma: adding channels improves reconstruction but makes generation harder.

## Key points

- We introduce APEX, short for Asynchronous Prefix denoising for EXtreme compression, which pairs a hierarchical latent space with asynchronous denoising over channel groups.
- Our autoencoder orders channel prefixes from structure to detail, while one shared denoiser generates them at staggered local times without adding tokens.
- On ImageNet-512, APEX outperforms existing methods at every evaluated channel count under 64x and 128x compression.
- At matched training and guided sampling compute, it also outperforms a 256-token model at every evaluated budget, with larger gains at lower compute.
