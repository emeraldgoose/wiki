# Investor's Last Exam: Can AI Agents Do Equity Investment Without Seeing the Future?

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.38342](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38342) · **Published**: 2026-10-28
**Authors**: Yuchen Cai

## Overview

Financial agents must form forecasts and investment judgments from decision-time evidence, with predictions evaluated against subsequent outcomes. Existing benchmarks cover financial knowledge, research workflows, or forecasting, but evaluating the full research process requires a strictly frozen information set and professional evaluation criteria. Investor's Last Exam (ILE) is an equity-research benchmark led by experienced buy-side investors and fund managers. Its three components are a versioned financial database spanning 3.43 million document versions and nine source categories, supplemented by structured market data with validated timestamps governing retrieval and computation; a task taxonomy organized around professional analyst deliverables; and an expert-led framework linking scoring criteria to sources, calculations, and assumptions through financial dependency graphs. Evaluation spans 86 assignments and jointly assesses research quality, forecast accuracy, and simulated investment performance. GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 lead overall at 68.1 and 66.9 out of 100, yet even Astra scores only 52.5 on Forecast & Model. The results show that even state-of-the-art models fail to translate overall research capability into predictive performance. ILE shifts the evaluation unit from isolated capabilities to the evidence-assumption-judgment chain, allowing researchers to locate where analytical advantages fail to become forecasts and investment judgments and providing a traceable, diagnostic foundation for training and evaluating financial agents.

## Key points

- ILE has a versioned database of 3.43 million document versions across 9 source categories.
- Validated timestamps govern retrieval and computation to avoid future leakage.
- Evaluation covers 86 assignments and research quality, forecast accuracy, and simulated investment performance.
- Top overall scores are GPT-6 Astra 68.1 and Claude Opus 5.5 66.9 out of 100.
- Even the top model scores only 52.5 on Forecast & Model.
- The benchmark traces analytical advantages through the evidence-assumption-judgment chain.

## Details

The benchmark is led by experienced buy-side investors and fund managers. Its financial dependency graphs link scoring criteria to sources, calculations, and assumptions so failures can be localized. By freezing information and grounding evaluation in professional deliverables, it avoids benchmarks that only test isolated financial knowledge.
