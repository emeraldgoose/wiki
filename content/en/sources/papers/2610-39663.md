# AB-Attention: Accurate Block Attention for Accelerating Video Diffusion Transformers

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.39663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39663) · **Published**: 2026-10-29
**Authors**: Bin Xu

## Overview

Video diffusion transformers generate high-quality videos, but the quadratic cost of 3D full attention dominates inference. Dynamic block-sparse attention reduces cost by computing exact attention only on key/value blocks selected from current activations. However, fidelity degrades under aggressive sparsity because blocks are scored from coarse summaries or costly global clustering, pruned blocks vanish from softmax, and whole tiles are kept or dropped even when only a few of their keys matter. Accurate Block Attention (AB-Attention) is a training-free method that accurately approximates dense attention at high sparsity. Its Intra-Block Clustering Selector estimates block importance faithfully yet cheaply by clustering queries only within each query block, without permuting tokens. Sampling with Inclusion-Probability Weighting then samples blocks in proportion to these estimates and reweights them by inverse inclusion probability, so one pass estimates dense attention rather than truncating it. Finally, a kernel gathers key/value blocks much smaller than a tile into full tensor-core tiles, refining selection at no extra tensor-core cost. On Wan2.1 and HunyuanVideo, AB-Attention skips 89-92% of attention, whereas baselines compute 1.8-3.6x as much, yet it improves PSNR by up to 4.4 dB over the strongest baseline and achieves up to 2.23x end-to-end speedup.

## Key points

- Dynamic block-sparse attention loses fidelity at aggressive sparsity.
- IBCS estimates block importance cheaply without token permutation.
- SIP samples blocks proportionally and reweights to estimate dense attention.
- Small K/V blocks are gathered into full tensor-core tiles.
- 89-92% of attention is skipped on Wan2.1 and HunyuanVideo.
- Baselines compute 1.8-3.6x more attention.
- AB-Attention improves PSNR by up to 4.4 dB and reaches 2.23x speedup.

## Details

The method avoids truncation by probabilistically estimating dense attention rather than dropping blocks. The three components separately handle importance estimation, sampling reweighting, and efficient tile gathering.
