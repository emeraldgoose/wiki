# DataVista: Diagnosing Multimodal LLMs on Data Video Understanding

**Source**: Xie et al., arXiv, 2026-10-08

**Lead**: Data video—animated charts woven into video narratives—is ubiquitous in news and business, yet no benchmark exists for it. DataVista fills this gap with 1,200+ questions across 300+ data videos, revealing that current multimodal LLMs struggle with the unique demands of reading animated charts, integrating evidence across time, and interpreting visual design as information.

## Background

Existing video benchmarks (ActivityNet, Something-Something) target general actions; chart benchmarks (ChartQA, PlotQA) target static images. Data video sits at the intersection: it requires reading *changing* data values across animation frames, linking chart evidence to spoken narration, and understanding how transitions, highlights, and layout guide the viewer's attention. None of the existing benchmarks capture this.

## DataVista Benchmark

**Construction**: 300+ data videos sourced from news outlets (BBC, NYT, Reuters), financial reports, and educational channels. Each video is 30–180 seconds with 3–15 animated charts.

**Annotation**: Expert annotators (data journalists, analysts) create questions in four capability tiers:
1. **Chart reading** (35%): Extract values, trends, comparisons from animated charts
2. **Cross-chart integration** (25%): Synthesize evidence across multiple charts/timepoints
3. **Narrative alignment** (20%): Link chart data to spoken/written narrative claims
4. **Design reasoning** (20%): Infer intent from visual choices (highlighting, transitions, layout)

**Models evaluated**: 12 multimodal LLMs including GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Qwen2.5-VL, InternVL2, LLaVA-Video, and open-source variants.

## Key Findings

| Model | Overall | Chart Reading | Cross-Chart | Narrative | Design |
|-------|---------|---------------|-------------|-----------|--------|
| GPT-4o | 62.3% | 71.2% | 58.1% | 60.4% | 54.7% |
| Claude 3.5 Sonnet | 59.8% | 68.9% | 54.3% | 58.7% | 52.1% |
| Gemini 1.5 Pro | 54.1% | 63.4% | 49.2% | 52.8% | 46.3% |
| Qwen2.5-VL-72B | 51.7% | 61.2% | 46.8% | 49.5% | 44.1% |
| Best open (InternVL2-76B) | 48.9% | 57.3% | 43.1% | 46.2% | 41.8% |

**Systematic failure modes**:
- **Temporal grounding**: Models hallucinate values at specific timestamps; frame sampling loses animation continuity
- **Cross-chart reasoning**: <40% accuracy on questions requiring evidence from 2+ charts
- **Design blindness**: Models treat visual design as decoration; cannot infer that a red highlight means "attention here"
- **Narrative-chart mismatch**: Models trust narration over visual evidence even when they contradict

## Relevance to Software Engineers

For teams building **data-driven video analytics**, **automated reporting**, or **financial video summarization**: DataVista exposes that current MLLMs cannot reliably "watch" a data video and answer analytical questions. The benchmark provides:
- A diagnostic test suite for chart-video understanding capabilities
- A taxonomy of failure modes to guide model improvement
- An evaluation protocol for video+chart multimodal models
