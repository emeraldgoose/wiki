# Order-Only On-Policy Distillation

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.02112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02112) · **Published**: 2026-11-01
**Authors**: Guangzeng Han, Yu Wu, Xiaolei Huang

## Overview

On-policy distillation trains a student on its own generations by matching the teacher's token probabilities. Those probabilities combine two kinds of information: which tokens the teacher prefers and how confident it is. Confidence reflects the teacher's capacity and calibration, which a smaller student may be unable to reproduce. Because teacher temperature changes magnitudes without changing ordering, OPD is also temperature-sensitive. The paper asks whether ranking candidate tokens is enough and proposes Order-Only On-Policy Distillation (O3PD), which uses only relative order information.

## Key points

- On-policy distillation matches teacher token probabilities.
- Probabilities mix preference with confidence.
- Smaller students may not reproduce teacher confidence.
- Teacher temperature changes probability magnitudes.
- Ordering is less sensitive than magnitude.
- Order-only distillation avoids relying on confidence.

## Details

The core question is whether confidence magnitude is necessary for distillation. The paper decomposes probabilities into preference and confidence, then argues that focusing on rank ordering reduces dependence on the teacher's calibration. By training on relative order rather than full probabilities, O3PD aims to be more robust to temperature choice and better suited to students with different capacities. The method targets knowledge transfer without forcing impossible calibration matches.
