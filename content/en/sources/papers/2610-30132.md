# LongZero: Enhancing Long-Context Reasoning in LLMs from Zero Data

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.30132](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30132) · **Published**: 2026-10-21
**Authors**: Zhennan Wan, Jianfei Chen

## Overview

Reinforcement learning strengthens long-context reasoning in LLMs but needs long, diverse, challenging, and verifiable training data that are costly to annotate and scarce in practice. Existing synthesis depends on external corpora or stronger teachers, which get harder to obtain as models improve. LongZero lets an LLM improve its own long-context reasoning without external data.

## Key points

- Targets long-context reasoning rather than short-context zero-data methods.
- Model first synthesizes question and answer with supporting evidence in compact structured form.
- Then expands evidence into a long multi-document context.
- Combines self-written multi-hop reasoning with self-written multi-round retrieval tasks.
- Trains with GRPO; outperforms baselines on five long-context reasoning benchmarks.

## Details

Zero-data self-generation avoids dependence on annotators, corpora, and teachers while still producing generalizable long-context reasoning improvements.
