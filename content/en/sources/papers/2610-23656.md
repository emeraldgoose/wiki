# ABF-Bench: A Verifiable Benchmark for Agentic Blockchain Forensics

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23656)
**Date**: 2026-10-16


## Overview

Blockchain forensics is adversarial and open-ended: money launderers split, swap, and bridge funds, cut their trail at mixers or custodial services, and change tactics from one incident to the next. Investigators must keep adapting their tracing in response. Yet the immutability of public ledgers preserves every recorded movement in public view, making much of the investigation independently verifiable. We introduce ABF-Bench, an interactive benchmark that makes evidence-backed fund tracing a verifiable agent task, in which every claimed movement must cite ledger records the agent retrieved. A Fund tracing determines where stolen value came from and where it went, and it is central to anti-money-laundering compliance and incident response on blockchains.
