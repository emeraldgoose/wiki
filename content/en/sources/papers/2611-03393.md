# TaoMate-FVSR:Audio-Guided Facial Video Restoration

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03393](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03393) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Xinyang Li, Zeran Xu, Qijun Gan, Chenwei Zhang, Meiguang Jin, Junfeng Ma

## Overview

AI-generated videos often depict people inside detailed scenes while leaving small facial regions poorly resolved. Visual super-resolution can sharpen these regions without ensuring that recovered lips and teeth agree with spoken audio. TaoMate-FVSR is an audio-guided facial video restoration framework that resolves this ambiguity within a reference-guided video flow model. Source-noise mixing initializes reconstruction from a partially preserved observation, and structured reference injection separates appearance anchors from continuous motion context. Training combines restoration and alignment objectives.

## Key points

- Generated videos often have under-resolved faces.
- Super-resolution may mismatch spoken audio.
- Audio guides facial restoration ambiguity.
- The model uses reference-guided video flow.
- Source-noise mixing initializes reconstruction.
- Appearance and motion context are separated.

## Details

The framework uses audio as a constraint on how facial details should match speech, rather than only improving visual sharpness. Structured reference injection differentiates stable identity appearance from motion-dependent context, which helps large facial reconstructions remain coherent over time. The combination of restoration and alignment objectives targets both visual quality and audio-visual agreement.
