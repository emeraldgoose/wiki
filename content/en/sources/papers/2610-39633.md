# Intrinsic Distribution Matching

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.39633](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39633) · **Published**: 2026-10-29
**Authors**: Deyuan Liu, Yansen Han, Kaiwen Zheng, Jun Xie, Jiacheng Liu, Zhenglin Cheng, Peng Sun, Jintao Zhang, Jianguo Li, Tao Lin

## Overview

One-step and few-step text-to-image generation must accurately render details specified in the prompt despite limited opportunities for refinement. Distribution matching distillation (DMD) uses score differences to bring the generated distribution closer to a reference distribution. Following prior work, the authors estimate this difference with internal velocity branches, avoiding a separate fake-score network. They ask whether an explicit moment constraint improves this learned score supervision. A decomposition of the reverse-KL field into a Gaussian moment component and a non-Gaussian residual identifies statistics that can be constrained explicitly. The paper introduces Intrinsic Distribution Matching (IDM), which retains the full DMD correction and matches feature means and per-coordinate standard deviations using the generator's exponential-moving-average copy, requiring no external feature encoder. Experiments use full-parameter and LoRA tuning on Z-Image-Turbo 6B and Qwen-Image 20B. At two network evaluations, IDM reaches 94.25% and 90.20% on Chinese and English LongText-Bench, the highest scores among compared methods at this budget. Controlled ablations show gains from the added moment loss, while generator EMA features perform competitively with an external DINOv2 encoder across five reported metrics.

## Key points

- IDM targets one- and few-step text-to-image generation quality.
- It retains full DMD correction while adding moment constraints.
- Features means and per-coordinate standard deviations are matched via generator EMA.
- No external feature encoder is required.
- IDM reached 94.25% Chinese and 90.20% English LongText-Bench.
- Ablations show gains from the added moment loss.
- Generator EMA features competed with DINOv2 across five metrics.

## Details

The moment constraint comes from decomposing the reverse-KL field into Gaussian moment and non-Gaussian residual components. By matching moments using the generator's own EMA, IDM avoids an external encoder while improving long-text rendering benchmarks.
