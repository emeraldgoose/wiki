# GLOW-FM: Efficient Generative Low-Light Image Enhancement with One-Step Latent Flow Matching

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.31222](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31222) · **Published**: 2026-10-22
**Authors**: Ariel Lapid, Yuval Haitman, Reuven Peretz, Arnon Netzer, Idit Diamant

## Overview

Low-light image enhancement (LLIE) recovers well-exposed and perceptually faithful images from degraded observations. Recent diffusion generative methods achieve strong perceptual quality but incur iterative sampling cost. GLOW-FM introduces one-step latent flow matching for efficient, high-quality enhancement under diverse real-world low-light conditions, aiming to reduce computation while maintaining perceptual fidelity.

## Key points

- Targets efficient single-evaluation LLIE.
- Uses latent flow matching instead of iterative diffusion sampling.
- Maintains perceptual quality across diverse lighting conditions.

## Details

The method focuses on one-step enhancement with low overall computation, making generative low-light restoration more practical for deployment.
