# LOLPlay: Benchmarking Real-Time Visual Agents in League of Legends

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03685) · **Published**: 2026-10-25
**Authors**: Zhaojian Yu

## Overview

Visual agents need to coordinate reasoning and action while their environment evolves during inference. LOLPlay is a benchmark in native League of Legends with 36 champion-specific tasks spanning resource management, combat, cooperation, and objectives. Agents interact through screenshots and mouse-keyboard actions, while automated initialization and 82 executable subgoal checks distinguish partial progress from completion. The benchmark evaluates how well visual models reason and act under rapid, interactive conditions that evolve beyond the agent's control.

## Key points

- The benchmark targets real-time coordination between perception and action.
- It uses 36 champion-specific tasks across four skill categories.
- Agents receive interactive control through screenshots and mouse-keyboard inputs.
- Automated initialization reduces manual setup variability.
- 82 executable subgoal checks separate partial progress from full completion.
- Additional reasoning effort did not improve metrics for any evaluated model.

## Details

Across 324 task evaluations of three models at three reasoning-effort settings, the GPT-6 Astra variant achieves the highest task success rate of 25.0 percent at low and medium effort, and the highest subgoal success rate of 40.3 percent at medium effort. High effort improves neither metric over low effort for any model. For the Astra system, longer requests leave fewer interaction rounds, suggesting a cost to additional reasoning in real-time control. Trajectories reveal incomplete subgoal composition and actions executing after the observed scene changes, motivating agents that combine visual reasoning with timely action.
