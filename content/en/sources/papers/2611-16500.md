# EvenVLM: A Text and Multimodal Dataset for the Even Language with Vision-Language Fine-Tuning

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16500)
**Published**: 2026-11-12

## Overview

EvenVLM presents the first text and multimodal (VQA) dataset for Even (Lamut), an endangered Northern Tungusic language spoken by approximately 6,800 people in Siberia. The dataset comprises 50,521 structured Even-Russian entries extracted from dictionary and phrasebook sources via GPT-4o Mini-based parsing, from which 107,543 training examples are synthetically generated covering translation, grammar, reading comprehension, and semantic classification.

## Key points

- **Tokenizer expansion**: a three-step algorithm adds Even-specific graphemes (eng, oe, o-umlaut), whitelisted vocabulary, and BPE merges — reducing average tokens per word by 27% (4.99 → 3.64).
- **LoRA fine-tuning of Qwen3-VL-4B**: text tasks (0.53% trainable parameters, rank 32) and visual question answering (rank 64, frozen vision encoder).
- **Best configuration**: 11.4% exact match and 13.6 weighted score on text benchmarks; 0.1283 chrF for sentence translation; **65% VQA accuracy** (up from 0% for the base model and text-only fine-tuning).
- **Released**: datasets, evaluation benchmarks, and training pipeline to support further research on Even and other low-resource languages.

## Details

Even's agglutinative morphology makes tokenization inefficient on standard BPE vocabularies; the three-step expansion directly addresses this. The frozen vision encoder with high LoRA rank (64) for VQA prevents catastrophic forgetting while adapting the language model for visual grounding in Even.
