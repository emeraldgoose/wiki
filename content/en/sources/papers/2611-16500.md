# EvenVLM: A Text and Multimodal Dataset for the Even Language with Vision-Language Fine-Tuning

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.16500](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16500) · **Published**: 2026-11-12
**Authors**: Daniil Makovetskii, Ivan Bondarenko

## Overview

Even (Lamut) is a Northern Tungusic language spoken by roughly 6,800 people — out of 19,900 who identify as Even — across the Magadan, Kamchatka, and Sakha regions of Siberia. It is classified as endangered, with sharply declining native speakers and limited intergenerational transmission, and almost no presence in NLP research. Two Novosibirsk State University authors present EvenVLM, the first text and multimodal (VQA) dataset for the language, together with a complete, reproducible pipeline for adapting a vision-language model to a low-resource agglutinative language. The work also addresses the ORACLE workshop's mission: evaluation tools in Even itself, rather than through translation to Russian, let indigenous communities assess model outputs in their own language.

The dataset starts from 50,521 structured Even–Russian entries extracted from dictionary and phrasebook sources via GPT-4o Mini-based parsing, synthetically expanded to 107,543 training examples spanning translation, grammar, reading comprehension, and semantic classification. The multimodal set adds 189 images with 477 bilingual annotations, producing 1,908 VQA examples.

Because standard tokenizers fragment low-resource agglutinative words excessively, the authors adapt the Qwen tokenizer with a three-step expansion: adding Even-specific special graphemes (ӈ, ө, ӫ), whitelisted vocabulary with BPE merges, and special character combinations. Average tokens per word drop 27%, from 4.99 to 3.64.

## Key points

- Dataset scale: 50,521 dictionary/phrasebook-derived Even–Russian entries → 107,543 synthetic training examples across four task families, plus 1,908 VQA examples from 189 images and 477 bilingual annotations — the first such resource for Even.
- Tokenizer expansion algorithm (special graphemes → whitelist + BPE merging → special character combinations) reduces average tokens per word by 27% (4.99 → 3.64), directly targeting fragmentation of Even's agglutinative morphology.
- Two-stage LoRA fine-tuning of Qwen3-VL-4B (a 4.4B-parameter VLM): stage 1 adapts text tasks with 0.53% trainable parameters at rank 32; stage 2 fine-tunes for VQA at rank 64 with the vision encoder frozen.
- Best configuration results: 11.4% exact match and a 13.6 weighted score on text benchmarks, 0.1283 chrF for sentence translation, and 65% accuracy on VQA — up from 0% for both the base model and text-only fine-tuning.
- All datasets, evaluation benchmarks, and the training pipeline are released in open access, with model weights to follow upon acceptance; the pipeline is designed to transfer to other low-resource agglutinative languages.

## Details

The paper situates itself against surveys of multimodal models for low-resource languages, which found fewer than ten papers applying VLMs to languages with under 10,000 speakers. Sections cover the Even language profile and source data, the extraction pipeline, template-based data synthesis, the tokenizer expansion algorithm and its results, and the two training stages with their LoRA configurations and training dynamics. Evaluation includes a benchmark suite with metrics, per-category VQA results, and qualitative observations on translation, classification, and VQA; an error analysis section closes the paper. The authors frame every first step — dataset, adaptation, evaluation — as valuable for documenting, preserving, and supporting Even, and position the pipeline as a template for culturally grounded multilingual assessment of vision-language models.
