# From Fragmentation to Fusion: A Survey and Roadmap for Omnimodal Embeddings

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.00172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00172) · **Published**: 2026-10-30
**Authors**: Ziyin Zhang, Yuyu Guo, Ying Zhang, Siqiao Xue, Zihan Liao, Jin Qin, Wenjie Yang, Siyuan Yang, Shijie Li, Cheng Chen, Hao Wu, Zhaoyang Zhu, Ruipeng Li, Zhaokun Jiang, Lei Lei, Rui Wang, Hang Yu, Rui Wang

## Overview

Embedding models underpin modern AI systems from search to recommendation. Despite shared mathematical foundations, embedding research is fragmented by modality, with communities developing largely in isolation. This survey argues that the fragmentation hides a deeper convergence. Contrastive learning, Transformer architectures, and a shift in granularity are pushing embedding methods across modalities toward common principles, even though modality-specific challenges remain underexplored. The paper surveys the field and proposes a roadmap toward unified omnimodal embeddings.

## Key points

- Embedding models support search, recommendation, and many AI applications.
- Modality communities remain somewhat isolated.
- Cross-modal methods still share mathematical foundations.
- Contrastive learning and Transformers drive convergence.
- A granularity shift is part of the unifying trend.
- Modality-specific challenges still need work.

## Details

The survey moves from a description of fragmentation to an argument for fusion. It identifies common design pressures across modalities while acknowledging that each modality has distinct data and evaluation issues. The roadmap proposes directions for omnimodal embedding systems that can operate across text, vision, audio, and other inputs. Rather than presenting one new model, the paper organizes cross-modal progress and points to shared future work.
