# MUON-RT Reusing the Teacher's Trajectory for Momentum-Free Muon for LLM Self-Distillation

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.41104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41104) · **Published**: 2026-10-30
**Authors**: Aryan Das, Vinay Kumar Verma, Aakansha Mishra

## Overview

Self-distillation methods for large language models with an exponential moving average teacher already retain a history of student updates, yet Muon still stores a separate momentum buffer, increasing persistent training memory. The paper proposes Muon with Reused Teacher (Muon-RT), which reuses the teacher-student displacement to eliminate momentum buffers for hidden weight matrices. It shows that this displacement encodes an exponentially weighted history of applied updates and smooths temporally uncorrelated noise under a local model. Muon-RT removes the radial component of the displacement, normalizes it spectrally, and mixes it with the current gradient after Newton-Schulz orthogonalization. Under explicit regularity and alignment assumptions, the authors establish linear contraction of an expected objective-gap bound toward a residual error level. With Self-Distillation Fine-Tuning on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Muon-RT improves average accuracy over AdamW by 2.6 points with 42% less persistent memory, and over momentum Muon by 5.4 points with 27% less persistent memory. With Self-Distillation Policy Optimization on Olmo-3-7B-Instruct, it improves average accuracy over AdamW by 2.0 points.

## Key points

- EMA-teacher self-distillation already retains update history, but Muon separately keeps momentum buffers that increase memory use.
- Muon-RT reuses the teacher-student displacement as an exponentially weighted history of updates and smooths temporally uncorrelated noise.
- The displacement is radially removed, spectrally normalized, and mixed with the gradient after Newton-Schulz orthogonalization.
- Theory shows linear contraction of an expected objective-gap bound to a residual level under stated regularity and alignment assumptions.
- SDFT on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct improves accuracy by 2.6 points over AdamW (~42% less memory) and 5.4 points over momentum Muon (~27% less memory); SDPO on Olmo-3-7B-Instruct gains 2.0 points.

## Details

The results separate memory savings from quality, suggesting that optimizer history can be shared with the EMA teacher in self-distillation settings rather than duplicated. Training setup, ablations, and proof assumptions are detailed in the linked source.
