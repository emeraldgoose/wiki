# Unlocking Any-Order Generation in Pretrained Autoregressive Image Models

**Source**: [huggingface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39293)
**Date**: 2026-10-29


## Overview

Autoregressive (AR) image generators are powerful in principle but inflexible in practice: training with a fixed raster-scan order hardcodes a single factorization of the image distribution into the model, leaving it unable to inpaint, outpaint, or edit at inference time. Existing solutions, including the modern discrete-diffusion family, train from scratch on a mixture of token orderings, paying roughly 3×3\times the compute of standard raster-scan training; discrete diffusion further sacrifices compatibility with the AR inference ecosystem (KV caching, vLLM) by construction.
