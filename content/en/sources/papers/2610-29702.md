# CausGround: Causal Interaction Grounding for Adaptive Embodied Planning

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.29702](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29702) · **Published**: 2026-10-21
**Authors**: Yijun Shen et al.

## Overview

Multimodal LLMs have promising reasoning but reliable physical interaction needs planning that accounts for when actions are executable and how they change the world. Preconditions and effects are environment-dependent and often missed by MLLMs. CausGround is a non-parametric causal interaction grounding framework that distills interaction experience into reusable action-centered causal knowledge, organized in a factorized world-state of object attributes and inter-object relations.

## Key points

- Builds reusable action preconditions and effects without parameter updates.
- Grounds task-relevant causal dependencies in current observations at planning time.
- Supports backward reasoning over preconditions and forward reasoning over effects.
- Improves vision-based high-level planning: 24.9% to 53.3% on adapted VirtualHome; 43.0% to 57.0% closed-loop planning on RoboCasa.
- Raises interaction reasoning accuracy from 25.8% to 31.9% on SWITCH; demonstrates real-world sequential manipulation.

## Details

Reusable causal knowledge lets embodied plans adapt across diverse environments without retraining, improving long-horizon planning.
