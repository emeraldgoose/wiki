# SLA: Scaling Spatial-Language-Action Model via Continual Pre-training

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.08373](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08373) · **Published**: 2026-11-05
**Authors**: Fuhao Li, Wenxuan Song, Qizhou Huang, Ziyang Zhou, Jingbo Wang, Junwu E, Pengwei Wang, Yan Wang, Shunbo Zhou, Haoang Li

## Overview

Reliable robotic manipulation requires both semantic understanding and precise control in three-dimensional space. Vision-language-action foundations largely inherit visual representations from 2D vision-language pre-training, while spatial supervision is often introduced only during task-specific adaptation. SLA treats spatial understanding as a transferable foundation-model capability learned from broader robot experience.

SLA initializes from a pretrained pi05 policy and continually pre-trains by jointly optimizing action prediction and alignment between intermediate visual embeddings and spatial representations from a frozen 3D perception model. It curates and unifies heterogeneous robot datasets across embodiments, tasks, and scenes to support spatial learning.

Feature-dependent gates adaptively aggregate visual embeddings from multiple VLA layers for spatial supervision. The frozen teacher exploits partially available depth and camera calibration alongside RGB, enabling effective use of heterogeneous geometric annotations. Probabilistic state dropout encourages reliance on visual cues and reduces overfitting to demonstrations. All auxiliary components are training-only, preserving the original inference architecture and pretrained VLA weights reuse.

## Key points

- Scales spatial understanding in VLA models through continual pre-training.
- Aligns visual embeddings with frozen 3D perception representations.
- Curates heterogeneous robot datasets across embodiments and scenes.
- Uses feature-dependent gates for layer aggregation.
- Leverages partial depth and calibration annotations.
- Exposes auxiliary components only during training.

## Details

The paper provides project and GitHub pages. It emphasizes direct reuse of pretrained VLA weights and inference architecture after training.
