# HubEgo-100K: In-the-Wild Egocentric Video of Skilled Work for Robot Learning

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21938)
**Published**: 2026-11-16

## Overview

Robot policies trained by imitation generalize poorly outside the environments and objects seen in training; published scaling studies find generalization grows with the diversity of training environments and objects rather than with more demonstrations per environment. Robot data is costly to collect across many environments, while egocentric human video can be recorded in the wild at scale — but existing egocentric video mostly shows daily-life activity and contains little skilled work in real workplaces.

HubEgo-100K is a dataset built for diversity. This paper covers the first release, v1.0: 10,124 hours of 1080p egocentric video with synchronized 100 Hz inertial (IMU) data from one head-mounted phone camera. In v1.0, 12,227 contributors recorded their own unscripted work in 15,548 environments across 11 setting types, 30 work categories, 56 task types and 35 action families, with 83% of hours in workplace settings. The release comprises 72,754 episodes with machine-generated action and language annotations, in LeRobot and MCAP formats under CC BY 4.0.

## Key points

- Scale and diversity: 10,124 h of 1080p video, 72,754 episodes, 12,227 contributors (none above 0.22% of hours; 2,824 contributors recorded ≥1 h) in 15,548 environments across 11 setting types; 82.6% of hours in workplace settings (including trades in homes, 26.9%), 17.4% in households; 30 work categories (manufacturing and assembly is 5.4% of hours), 56 task types, 35 action families.
- Hand visibility and on-task share: model-estimated at least one hand visible in 94.7% of sampled frames (both hands 76.3%); hours-weighted on-task share 96.9%.
- Open-set growth inside v1.0: with episodes in random order, new contributors and new words keep appearing, i.e. coverage does not saturate within the release.
- Episodes pair every video frame with per-frame phone IMU data (100 Hz) plus task types and machine-generated action and language annotations — an inertial signal absent from most prior egocentric corpora.
- The paper relates measurements to published evidence on six data properties tied to policy performance, including hand visibility and inertial sensing; it documents collection, curation, privacy measures, data format and a datasheet. Later versions will add benchmarks and further releases toward 100,000 hours.

## Details

- Collection: contributors record their own unscripted work via a head-mounted phone camera (single viewpoint); curation pipeline, annotation (machine-generated action/language), privacy/ethics handling, and data format (LeRobot and MCAP) are documented; release under CC BY 4.0 with a datasheet.
- Positioning vs prior datasets: unlike Ego4D (daily life), EPIC-KITCHENS (kitchens), Ego-Exo4D (123 scene contexts, multi-view rig) or Egocentric-10K/100K (factory/manual labor, no per-frame IMU or task/action annotations at this breadth), HubEgo-100K spans 30 work categories led by skilled trades and pairs video with inertial data and annotations.
