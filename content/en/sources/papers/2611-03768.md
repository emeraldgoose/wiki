# Multi-layer Indexer: Overlapping Selection and Attention in DeepSeek Sparse Attention

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.03768](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03768) · **Published**: 2026-11-02
**Authors**: Serdarcan Dilbaz, Arjun Krishna, Kiran Kamble, Daniel M. Bikel

## Overview

Sparse attention cuts long-context inference cost by making the attention matrix sparse: each query attends only a subset of the sequence, chosen by a fixed pattern or, in recent production LLMs, learned per query. In DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA), used by DeepSeek-V3.2 and the GLM-5 family, every layer is preceded by a lightning indexer that scores the whole cache and keeps the top 2048 tokens for the layer to attend. At long context, this indexer scan, not the sparse attention it feeds, dominates decode time. Adjacent layers nevertheless select nearly the same tokens, so a layer can attend the selection produced by the previous indexer layer instead of its own. This removes the dependency, letting each indexer run concurrently with the attention of its layer. The resulting Multi-layer Indexer (MLI) needs no retraining and no new weights. Its default realization changes no kernel and runs inside SGLang's production decode graph; an optional fused decode kernel accelerates it further on DeepSeek-V3.2. MLI composes with IndexCache (cross-layer reuse shipped as IndexShare in GLM-5.2): IndexCache drops the indexer from some layers, and MLI hides the scan on the rest. Evaluated on DeepSeek-V3.2 and GLM-5 under IndexCache's protocol at retentions 1/2 to 1/32 and contexts 10K to 195K, at equal retention the composition decodes faster than IndexCache, up to 14.5% faster on DeepSeek-V3.2 and 9.8% on GLM-5 at 1/2, 7.9% and 5.1% at 1/4, largest where IndexCache keeps the most indexers and growing with context. At retention 1/r it is faster still than IndexCache at 1/(2r) while retaining twice the fresh selections; at 128K it reaches 1.32x throughput at 1/4 retention versus IndexCache's 1.28x at 1/8. LongBench, RULER, and LongBench v2 accuracy remain within a few percentage points of IndexCache's.

## Key points

- In DSA, the per-layer lightning indexer scan becomes the decode-time bottleneck at long context despite sparse attention.
- Adjacent layers usually select nearly the same tokens.
- MLI lets a layer reuse the previous indexer's selection, removing the dependency and enabling overlap with attention.
- No retraining or new weights; default path runs in SGLang without kernel changes, with an optional fused kernel.
- At equal retention it beats IndexCache by up to 14.5% on DeepSeek-V3.2 and 9.8% on GLM-5, with accuracy within a few points.

## Details

Experiments cover retentions from 1/2 to 1/32 and contexts from 10K to 195K on DeepSeek-V3.2 and GLM-5. Composition with IndexCache trades fresh selection against retained selections. Full benchmark numbers and kernel details are in the linked source.
