## Overview

Recent video super-resolution (VSR) models achieve efficient one-step generation through diffusion distillation. However, high-resolution inference remains costly, and distilled models are difficult to align with human preferences. These challenges call for improvements in both model architecture and training strategy. We present FlashVSR+ to address both limitations. On the architecture side, we introduce hybrid attention based on the strong locality observed in pretrained VSR models, while retaining sparse global interaction for long-range consistency.
