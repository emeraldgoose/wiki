# Learning Quality-Controllable Robot Policies with Iterative Hindsight Relabeling

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.37393](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37393) · **Published**: 2026-10-27
**Authors**: Sinjae Kang, Dong Kyu Shin, Jaehong Kim, Kamin Lee, Hyoeun Kim, Kaixin Wang, Li Zhao, Seonghyeon Ye, Kimin Lee

## Overview

Robot policy evaluation often relies on a binary success signal, while real-world behaviors are also defined by quality attributes such as execution speed and motion smoothness that must be dynamically controlled. The paper introduces Quality-Controllable Policy (QCP), enabling inference-time control over individual and combined quality attributes. To extend controllable quality range beyond initial demonstrations, QCP uses an iterative hindsight relabeling loop: collect online rollouts under target labels, add successful trajectories, relabel accumulated trajectories by measured quality, and fine-tune. Experiments show improved execution quality and success rates, including real-world manipulation gains.

## Key points

- Extends robot policies from binary success to quality controllability.
- Uses iterative hindsight relabeling to expand quality range.
- Demonstrates real-world deployment benefits.

## Details

QCP enables speed and stability control according to task requirements through hindsight quality relabeling.
