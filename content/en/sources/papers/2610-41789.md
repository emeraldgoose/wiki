# Hard Sample-Conditioned Decorrelated Attention for Robust Face Recognition

**Source**: [arXiv:2610.41789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41789) · **Published**: 2026-10-30
**Authors**: Tahar Chettaoui

## Overview

Multi-head attention in Vision Transformers permits independent attention patterns, but heads may become redundant without explicit regularization. Correlation-based penalties can encourage disentanglement, yet uniform regularization ignores differences in sample difficulty that matter in face recognition, where hard samples near competing identities drive learning and generalization.

ViT-HarDis conditions attention-head disentanglement on sample difficulty. Samples with high cosine similarity to negative class centers receive stronger head decorrelation, enforcing specialization where identity ambiguity is highest. The method requires no architectural changes or additional inference cost.

Experiments on most benchmarks show improvements over baseline and uniform disentanglement. Trained on WebFace4M, ViT-HarDis achieves state-of-the-art results among Vision-Transformer-based methods on IJB-B, IJB-C, and TinyFace Rank-1. With MS1MV3 training it achieves the best IJB-B and TinyFace results.

## Key points

- Conditions head decorrelation on sample difficulty rather than applying uniform regularization.
- Strengthens decorrelation for samples close to competing class centers.
- Improves robustness on most evaluated face recognition benchmarks.
- Reduces similarity of hard samples to competing class centers and lowers inter-head attention similarity.
- Applies disentanglement only to final transformer layers for better results than regularizing the whole network.
- Adds no architecture or inference overhead.

## Details

The method also reports that selectively regularizing the final transformer layers outperforms regularizing the entire network. Code is provided, and the paper is accepted at WACV 2027.
