# Decoupled Predictive Decision Making: A Shared Conditional Predictor for Agents

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.02075](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02075) · **Published**: 2026-11-01
**Authors**: Lang Mo, Xingxuan Zhang, Qi Wang, Mingchao Hao, Hao Yuan, Chunjing Gan, Wang Lu, Peng Cui

## Overview

Agent decisions often depend on empirical relationships that deployment observations reveal and task descriptions alone cannot resolve. Decoupled Predictive Decision Making formulates heterogeneous agent decisions as conditional prediction with a shared pretrained learner.

Language models provide semantic representations of current and historical records. A general-purpose conditional predictor then predicts task-defined targets from that evidence. Pretraining supplies reusable inference capability, while deployment evidence instantiates problem-specific predictions without updating neural parameters. Tool judgments, candidate preferences, and reasoning assessments share an evidence-and-query interface, and predictions are mapped to choices by a decision rule.

With Qwen3.5-27B as the semantic encoder, DPDM raises mean accuracy across 50 benchmarks from 78.80% to 84.92% under evaluated history budgets. On ToolPRMBench tasks, absolute gains are 34.26% and 28.87%. Accuracy improves as history grows, and balanced accuracy improves with imbalanced histories.

## Key points

- Decouples semantic knowledge from empirical inference capability.
- Uses a shared conditional predictor across heterogeneous agent decisions.
- Predicts without updating model parameters at deployment.
- Maps predictions to choices through a decision rule.
- Improves tool judgments, candidate preferences, and reasoning assessments.
- Demonstrates gains that grow with available interaction history.

## Details

The work defines complementary sources of agent capability: semantic knowledge from language pretraining and empirical inference from structured-data pretraining. A single frozen LimiX-2 predictor serves five task categories, with results across 50 benchmarks.
