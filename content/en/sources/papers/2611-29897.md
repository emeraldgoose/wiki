# Motion Mamba 2.0: Autoregressive Diffusion Mamba for Long-Horizon Humanoid Control

**Source**: [arXiv:2611.29897](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29897) · **Published**: 2026-11-23 (v1)
**Authors**: Yue Huang, Tianshan Zhang, Zeyu Zhang, Jayeon Kang, Akide Liu, Sungjoon Choi, Dacheng Tao, Hao Tang

## Overview

Interactive humanoid control needs a generator that keeps responding to changing text instructions and spatial goals while motion is already in progress — a setting where autoregressive diffusion suffers from two coupled problems: bounded attention caches drop distant history, and repeated window denoising is slow. Motion Mamba 2.0 reformulates the authors' ECCV 2024 Motion Mamba into an autoregressive root–body diffusion architecture for the Unitree G1. It generates successive 52-frame windows (2.08 s at 25 Hz); explicit root predictions drive local trajectory features for body generation, and inside both denoisers local attention is combined with a resumable Mamba-3 recurrent branch.

The key representation is persistent content memory (PCM): it maps compressed Mamba recurrent-state summaries, their successive state changes, and text-conditioned features into gated content slots that local attention can read, giving attention direct access to compressed long-range history alongside the sliding cache. An accepted-only history update separates diffusion sampling from physical time: intermediate noisy candidates read an immutable history snapshot, and the cache, recurrent states, and PCM slots are committed together only after a clean window is accepted. For inference, an execution scheme combining execution-flow simplification, a fused attention/denoising kernel, and CUDA Graph replay across the three classifier-free-guidance branches preserves the canonical history update while cutting latency.

## Key points

- Long-horizon control on retargeted HiPHI (1/4/9/19 windows = 2.08–39.52 s): lowest Root CE at every horizon — 2.78 cm at 2.08 s, 7.55 cm at 39.52 s (vs 7.78 cm MotionStreamer) — and lowest or tied End CE — 13.64 cm at 39.52 s (8.1% below ARDY's best 14.84 cm); at 18.72 s, 2.87/8.45 cm vs MotionStreamer 6.17/18.07 cm and ARDY 8.15/11.06 cm. Highest Cmd. and R@3 at all four horizons (46.05% / 23.68% at 39.52 s); lowest L2-FID at 8.32/18.72/39.52 s.
- Short-horizon: on BONES Seed at 8.32 s, Root CE 1.86 cm vs ARDY 4.96 cm (-62.5%), End CE 6.86 vs 10.16 cm, highest R@3 35.58%, lowest L2-FID 0.459; on retargeted HumanML3D at 8.32 s, Root/End CE 2.17/7.03 cm vs ARDY 4.75/9.82 cm (-54.3% Root CE) and highest 4-window R@3 49.72%. Single-window text retrieval (R@3 84.29% on HumanML3D) trails MotionStreamer (96.40%), so precise spatial control and single-window retrieval are not uniformly best.
- Ablations at 39.52 s on HiPHI: adding the Mamba branch cuts Root CE 60.00→9.36 cm (End CE 64.86→15.54); adding PCM further cuts Root CE to 7.55 (-19.3%) and End CE to 13.64, lifting Cmd. 32.20→46.05% and R@3 17.44→23.68%; removing PCM text conditioning gives 8.62/14.62 cm, removing the state-delta feature gives 8.30/14.18 cm.
- Inference efficiency on one RTX 4090 (batch 1, 10 DDIM steps): median complete-window latency 1685.35→276.14 ms (p95 2471.65→345.82 ms), median paired speedup 6.138x (95% CI [6.094, 6.290]), real-time factor 0.133 for a 2.08 s window — with identical outputs and committed states in the tested rollouts.
- Real-world deployment on a physical Unitree G1: sequential text-guided action composition, expressive motion, and interactive keyboard/mouse locomotion, with 94% full-task success across 100 trials and no observed falls; rollouts tracked with SONIC.
- Training: AdamW, 100,000 updates, global batch 512, lr 1e-6→5e-5 over 500 updates then cosine to 1e-5, grad clip 1.0; data mixture BONES Seed / retargeted HumanML3D / retargeted HiPHI at 70:15:15 with history/future length sampling up to 196 frames (7.84 s); evaluation uses 10-step deterministic DDIM (eta=0), guidance scales (2,2), 128 held-out initialization families x 3 noise seeds = 384 rollouts per horizon. Code: github.com/AIGeeksGroup/MotionMamba2.0.

## Details

Each trial specifies a root target plus one wrist or ankle target with a terminal deadline, available from rollout onset; only the active text instruction is given during generation. Metrics: Root CE = horizontal root position error in the world X-Z plane (cm); End CE = 3D error of the constrained wrist/ankle at the deadline (cm); Cmd. = coarse root-motion state agreement; R@3 = text-motion retrieval with frozen evaluators (32 candidates on HiPHI; 1 generated + 31 fixed distractors elsewhere, comparable only within a benchmark); L2-FID on L2-normalized 256-d motion features of phase-paired 52-frame windows; latency per accepted window at batch size 1. PCM slots are not designed to reproduce arbitrarily distant instructions verbatim, and fixed-size state/cache/slots bound stored context. The efficiency study holds weights frozen and changes only execution: flow simplification (no unused pending-state materialization), fused kernel, and CUDA Graph replay, measured over 30 matched window pairs in each of five processes with 10,000-block bootstrap CIs.
