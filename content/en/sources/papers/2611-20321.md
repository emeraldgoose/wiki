# Unreasonable Confidence from Simulator Misspecification: Directional Robustness on SPEED+

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20321)
**Published**: 2026-11-15

## Overview

Vision-based spacecraft navigation must be trained almost entirely on synthetic imagery, because labelled real images of a satellite in orbit essentially do not exist. SPEED+ pairs ~60,000 labelled synthetic images of the Tango spacecraft with ~9,500 real images of a physical mockup lit two ways: lightbox (diffuse, Earth-albedo-like) and sunlamp (collimated solar simulator, harsh directional light). The synthetic images are OpenGL rasterisations whose radiometric formula stands in for uncomputed light physics — so the simulator's error is radiometric (missed specular highlights, hard shadows, sensor saturation), not geometric; range distributions match across domains.

Using a deliberately simple monocular pose estimator (ImageNet ResNet-50 → pooled 2048-d features → two-layer MLP head regressing translation and a sign-invariant quaternion rotation), the author runs a 2×2 study of backbone adaptation (frozen vs last-stage layer-4 trained) × photometric augmentation, on 28,000 synthetic training images, tested on both real domains (lightbox 6,740; sunlamp 2,791; no real image used for training/tuning/selection), seeds 42/43/44. Every post-review experiment was pre-registered; code, per-image predictions and the pre-registration record are on GitHub.

Three findings stand out. (1) The model does not know it is failing: split-conformal regions calibrated to 90% on fresh synthetic images cover only 87.7–91.4% in-domain but collapse to 9–15% of rotation errors on sunlamp and 21–38% on lightbox — a wrong pose carries the confidence of a correct one. Restoring 90% sunlamp rotation coverage would require growing the attitude cone ~5–6× (≈78% of SO(3)). (2) Transfer is asymmetric: scaling to 28k images and 2048-d pooled features improved synthetic rotation error 1.65 → 1.03 rad but moved sunlamp by only ~0.05 rad (under twice seed s.d.), while layer-4 adaptation carried 63–83% of its extra synthetic gain to both real domains. (3) Once the backbone adapts, augmentation helps only the direction it models: the photometric stack improved lightbox 1.166 → 0.987 rad on every seed but moved sunlamp within seed-to-seed variation (+0.116/−0.265/−0.047 paired deltas); with the backbone frozen the order reverses and augmentation helps sunlamp more (with frozen backbone, lightbox 1.185 and sunlamp 1.876).

## Key points

- Baseline honesty first: an un-canonicalised quaternion MSE (double cover) left training loss at 0.892 ≈ dataset-mean prediction; hemisphere canonicalisation alone dropped it to 0.285 and validation rotation error to 1.666 rad. Memorisation gap measured at ~0.40 rad (0.654 train vs ~1.05 val, seed 42).
- Main table (mean ± s.d., chance 2.207 rad): frozen+no-aug 1.763/2.004 (lightbox/sunlamp); frozen+aug 1.708/1.876; layer-4 no-aug 1.166/1.534; layer-4 + aug 0.987/1.468 — synthetic reference unchanged by augmentation (0.317 → 0.322 rad).
- Augmentation is directional: helps the diffuse domain it models once the backbone adapts; sunlamp translation pushed to the no-skill line (two of three augmented seeds fail to beat the fitted constant 0.372).
- Pre-registration: every post-review experiment (frozen-backbone block, frozen+aug, augmentation ablation, AdaBN, image statistics, constant-predictor baseline) is in the pre-registration record with deviations and corrections disclosed; the paper is explicit about what was not pre-registered.
- Failure analysis (Sec. 7) and normalisation statistics (Sec. 8) show the errors concentrate where appearance departs from the renderer — specular foil and hard shadows — not where geometry is hard.

## Details

- Setup: 28,000 train / 2,000 val synthetic (320×512 grayscale); conformal calibration/test on two disjoint 1,000-image sets excluding those 2,000; full real domains as test; Adam lr 1e-3, batch 16, 20 epochs, checkpoint by best synthetic val rotation error; frozen arm caches pooled features (seeds 43/44 retrain head on cache).
- Model/loss: L = MSE(t̂,t) + λ(1−⟨q̂,q⟩²), λ=1; q̂ ℓ2-normalised; sign-invariant squared inner product + hemisphere canonicalisation remove the quaternion double cover.
- Metrics: translation E_T (range-normalised), geodesic rotation E_R = 2·arccos|⟨q̂,q⟩|, SPEED+ score S = E_T + E_R (reported as plain sum, no SPEC2021 thresholding).
- Limitations (Sec. 10): ratios flatter weak models (synthetic-to-real "gap" can narrow by the reference getting worse — absolute errors are reported instead); single satellite mockup; one simple estimator; augmentation cannot fix radiometric misspecification outside its modeled direction.
