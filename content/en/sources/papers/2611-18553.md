# WeightBench: Evaluating Language Models as Weight-Space Programmers

**Source**: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18553)
**Published**: 2026-11-13

## Overview

WeightBench (University of Southern Mississippi) is a benchmark for Weight-Space Programming (WSP): the ability of large language models to operate directly on the parameters of other neural networks — reading what a network was trained to do, fixing a network that fails, and writing working networks from scratch, all without gradient descent. It evaluates LLMs of increasing capability on 42 WSP tasks across five tracks — Static Diagnosis (READ), Surgical Patching (FIX), Infeasibility Verification (META), Zero-Shot Weight Synthesis (WRITE), and Hardware-Aware Optimization (SYSTEMS) — over subject networks ranging from 2K-parameter control policies to 7B-parameter language models.

The framework couples a strict execution environment and auditing layer (sandboxed execution with guard injection, operator metering, data-flow provenance, audit flags and quarantine logic, red-team-audited) with procedural task scaling and contamination mitigation, so agents cannot bypass WSP constraints through brute-force trial and error. Results show clear scaling behavior among fully evaluated models, and that current models are still in the early phase of genuine weight-space programming.

## Key points

- Macro WeightBench scores: Claude Opus 5.5 (0.535) > Sonnet (0.466) > Haiku (0.402); DeepSeek v4-flash lowest overall (0.215) with the highest quarantine rate (13 of 42 runs).
- GPT-6.1-sol (run on 38 tasks) scores 0.568 on shared tasks vs Opus 0.546, with best TR-01 (0.928) and TR-04 (0.863) means, but well below the Claude models on TR-03 (0.260).
- TR-04 (Weight Synthesis) separates models most: Opus 0.829, Sonnet 0.569, Haiku 0.369, DeepSeek 0.000, GPT-6.1-sol 0.863 — zero-shot weight synthesis from scratch is real but uneven.
- TR-03 (Infeasibility Verification): the three Claude models score 0.485–0.500 on every task — essentially random-guessing baseline; models cannot distinguish blocked vs missing capability from weights alone.
- TR-05 (Hardware-Aware Optimization): all agents poor, 0.177–0.238 — data-free, non-privileged compression remains very hard.
- TR-02 (Surgical Patching) remains largely open: agents largely fail targeted behavioral fixes without collateral damage; on four reasoning-loop tasks only Opus (three) and Sonnet (one) score above zero.

## Details

- Tracks: READ (static diagnosis of weights), FIX (surgical patching), META (infeasibility verification — recognize impossible edits), WRITE (zero-shot weight synthesis), SYSTEMS (hardware-aware, data-free compression/optimization).
- Auditing: strict compute auditing, isolated execution environment, hard edit budgets; operator metering and data-flow provenance trace how outputs were produced; audit flags quarantine non-compliant runs; red-team audit validation included.
- Contamination mitigation: procedural task scaling with generators and anti-memorization measures, so memorized solutions do not transfer across instances.
- Task validity and baselines are validated before agent evaluation; macro scores are reported across the five tracks with per-track breakdowns.
- Conclusion: LLMs can map natural-language semantics directly to the geometry of network parameters (zero-shot control-policy synthesis confirms this), but lack the precision to surgically patch issues or safely compress large language models without catastrophic collateral damage; WSP is in its infancy.
- Outlook: the authors plan complete evaluations of additional models (e.g., Claude Fable) and dynamic difficulty scaling, positioning WeightBench as the proving ground for maturing WSP into an engineering discipline.
